## Canonical execution and authority

SupplyContracts is owner-only cadence configuration, not bilateral funding. CompanyWorkflows requires already accepted delivery/lease/finance contexts. This feature therefore negotiates compatible terms of one existing, actually funded GIN depot order, then invokes the existing `Delivery.acceptDelivery` once. It creates no synthetic workflow, escrow, money, goods, loan, fee, procurement purchase, or automation grant.

Each order permits one negotiation, at most three alternating offers, and at most three simultaneously live proposals per original account. Quantity and procurement cash cap are negotiable within canonical delivery limits. Existing unit price and the original absolute delivery deadline are fixed and explicitly labeled in the DTO. Offer expiry is bounded and leaves at least one minute for canonical acceptance. Acceptance pays nothing; canonical procurement, delivery, receipt, expiry, and money movement remain separate existing game actions. The accepted supplier spend-limit declaration is passed unchanged to canonical delivery; no claim is made that this feature controls the supplier's independent purchases or guarantees fulfillment/profit.

Both parties consent through their own authenticated current characters: initial buyer offer, opposite-party counter or acceptance. `G.withTwoCharacters` owns character/account transactions and stable pair locks. The caller then locks owned depot, latest policy, existing order, and negotiation last. Original account/character, depot/order, fixed dates, source digest, last offerer, exact terms, and revision are bound into the offer digest. The digest recursively sorts JSON keys so native JSONB ordering cannot invalidate identity. All transitions use conditional current-state/revision/hash updates. Fresh account status, ownership, order price/remaining units/expiry, exact source binding and database deadline are reread after locks. Buyer agent actions invoke the exact existing `guardDelegatedControl`; a current policy prevents delegated buyer configuration authority. Supplier agents retain ordinary canonical delivery permissions. No owner session flag can be supplied in a public body.

Funding requires exactly one canonical restock-escrow and fee journal, negative safe integer cash deltas, correct depot/order binding, sufficient original backing for remaining order quantities, and receipt timestamps between source creation and current database time. The immutable source digest binds journal IDs/content and current policy identity/revision/expiry. Existing canonical reservations remain authoritative at acceptance. Unknown fields, forged success claims, foreign parties, inactive accounts, wrong price, stale version/hash, source drift, and excessive quantities/rounds are refused.

Withdrawal/expiry close metadata and do not refund or debit existing funding. Canonical cancellation owns refunds. Historical source IDs intentionally have no foreign keys into volatile order/commitment rows; canonical estate may delete them without being blocked. Pending negotiations become effectively expired/dead, and either original account can close their metadata. Original-account heirs can read and replay historical receipts, with no new reservation, funding, inherited party consent, or capital. Private board data is visible only to the two original accounts and includes agreed caps rather than private balances. No customer notifications or provider messages are introduced.
