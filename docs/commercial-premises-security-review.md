Verified backing is precisely an original account's established estate (company key equals account, canonical tier >=1), or its locally owned street deed (canonical key/district match and `onchain_token_id IS NULL`). A contested street controller does not confer ownership. An estate has no fabricated district/address; a street exposes its actual game district. An optional original-character-owned open depot contributes a separately labeled `businessDistrict`, without claiming that district is the estate's location. Backing, ownership and original living parties are rechecked when accepting a lease or accessing its business slot.

One persistent offer per landlord account prevents parallel offers bypassing the two-slot cap. Active occupancy is canonical lease state with unique `(offer_id,slot)` backing and finite term (60 seconds to one day), not an informal company note. Tenants hold at most two live leases. Rent is bounded integer game cash ($1–$100,000) transferred immediately from the tenant's existing cash to the original landlord's cash, with exactly paired canonical ledger rows. There is no escrow, unbacked rent, refund faucet, external currency, provider call or new money bucket. `assertPremisesOccupancy` supplies the locked canonical occupancy guard for future gameplay consumers; `commercialPremisesAccess` supplies participant-scoped read authorization.

Mutations require canonical sorted living-character/account-persistent locks; the core additionally locks involved `accounts` rows in sorted order to share `setAgentCompany`'s owner mutex. Leaves are optional depot → sorted company profiles → canonical estate/deed row → offer → lease. Existing deed extraction shares account-persistent and deed locks; profile changes share the accounts/profile mutex. Request identity is original account/action/key plus exact normalized closed terms; replay precedes new eligibility and peer discovery. Offer/profile revisions are checked under locks, and CAS writes require one affected row. Expiry is refreshed after lock acquisition. Explicit lease consent and quoted maximum rent prevent silent repricing. Humans and agent credentials exercise ordinary signed gameplay only; no owner policy or external spending authority is changed.

Public offers require offer publication and published company consent, rechecked after profile reload. Their bounded DTO omits property keys, accounts, private estate/deed names, addresses, tenants and partner data. Owner/tenant boards and live access are account scoped; only original living participants receive live premises access. Offer withdrawal stops intake while preserving prepaid occupancy. A landlord cannot cancel a valid unexpired paid lease or shrink/remove an occupied valid slot; a tenant may surrender with no refund. Expiry allows slot reuse. Actual ownership/premises drift invalidates live access; repricing alone preserves existing lease terms. Active valid occupancy also blocks switching the optional depot to evict a tenant.

Death preserves legitimate account-owned estate/deed rights, already documented in canonical property tables, while invalidating original-character offers/leases through `voidCommercialPremisesAtDeath(client,victim)`. Paid rent is already landlord pocket wealth and follows the canonical pocket estate burn/loot rules. This hook writes no cash or new inheritance. A successor can replay original-account receipts, then publish a fresh verified property offer with new consent/revision; it cannot inherit an old tenant's occupancy or regain paid rent through a refund.
