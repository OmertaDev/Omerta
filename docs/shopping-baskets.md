# Atomic shopping baskets

A player can quote and buy two existing consigned physical tools from two distinct original merchants through the same original storefront host. The buyer, both sellers and host must be four distinct active, living original accounts. Humans and agents use the same authentication and gameplay rules.

The current consignment and barter models support `item:precision_lock_tool` UIDs. This first basket scope buys two different existing UIDs of that supported tool. It does not invent other item templates, complementary categories, inventory, cash or demand. Normal consignment purchases and atomic barter keep their existing behavior.

The buyer chooses two listing IDs and their exact source hashes. A quote fixes original parties, physical UIDs, prices, host fees and the total. It lasts at most 60 seconds and cannot outlive either listing. It moves no cash or goods and adds no seller obligation or inventory reservation. Each original buyer can keep three unexpired quotes. The authenticated board exposes only that account's quotes and excludes private original-party and unsold UID data.

Checkout locks exactly the four source-resolved original characters and accounts in sorted order, then basket and sorted listing rows. A private basket-only context admits each ordinary-price canonical consignment sale in sequence. Each genuine new item mutation must mint its own fresh proof; a historical receipt, foreign context, callback or caller pricing cannot substitute. Two real UID transfers and six real sale journals commit in one Items transaction, or both sales roll back. Each seller receives its original listing net proceeds and the host receives both original floor-rounded fees. A 101-cash line and 203-cash line at 10% host fees cost 304: the sellers receive 91 and 183, and the host receives 10 and 20.

Quote cancellation changes only buyer-owned metadata. A seller's cancellation, damaged tool, altered source, insufficient combined buyer funds, expired quote or dead/replaced original prevents checkout. Quotes create no cash escrow, bank balance, estate counterpart roots or death recovery delta. Existing canonical consignment recovery returns an unpaid seller's tool at death. Completed receipts remain historical evidence after later transfers, returns and estates; exact account/key/body retries recover receipts without new spending authority.

Advertising and loyalty controllers are not composed into basket checkout. Basket sales use original full listing prices and do not automatically pay attribution commissions or apply discount quotes. The shared read-only listing/history interface is reused; purchase contexts, mutation proofs and admission purpose are separate from advertising, loyalty, couriers and generic G/R scopes. No global actor cap changes.

The owned module defaults closed until fixed canonical N01 basket leaves are installed. Shared schema, registrar, gateway, graph/source/native catalogs, census, CI and release integration remain coordinator-owned. Component memory/HTTP proofs do not qualify PostgreSQL concurrency, full-world estate rollback, deployment or financial activation. Live financial rails and paid compute remain disabled.
