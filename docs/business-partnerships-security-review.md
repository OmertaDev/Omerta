## Approved semantics and cash attribution

Two original, living ordinary company-account characters separately consent to immutable terms. Mandatory flags declare `contributionAtRisk=true`, `distributionMode='voluntary_realized_gin_profit'`, `noPrincipalRefund=true`, and `noOwnerAuthority=true`. The latter grants no new owner actions to the partner; the original depot owner's normal control remains intact. No guaranteed dividend, refund, equity share, transferable asset, or generic agency permission is created. Each participant funds their exact agreed cash amount once, within 100000; one live agreement per owned depot and a bounded six-hour lifetime apply.

Owner capital invokes existing `Depot.fundDepot`. Partner capital first debits their real pocket and credits the original owner with paired `G.ledger` cash legs, then the canonical funding function immediately debits that owner pocket into actual depot treasury. Both original characters/accounts are held by ordinary sorted `G.withTwoCharacters`; no intermediate owner credit is exposed outside the transaction. Funding records bind unique canonical fund journal IDs, amounts, original actors, and actual partner/owner paired cash legs. They are bookkeeping evidence, not another money bucket.

Only the original owner may choose a distribution. The source is realized canonical GIN retail sale cash plus negative cost-of-goods delta, minus all supported post-activation journal expenses; capital, escrow, refunds, ordinary owner withdrawals, and other income never become profit. Unknown reasons, malformed/future source data, unsupported or over-200 source windows fail closed. Activation begins only after both actual full contributions. Funding lookup and payout-source windows are bounded. The chosen amount is limited by real earned cumulative profit less prior actual distributions, immutable lifetime cap, and treasury above the maximum canonical/declared/current-policy reserve.

Existing `Depot.withdrawDepot` releases the actual selected cash, then a real paired transfer pays the original partner. Cumulative integer floor allocates the fixed basis-point share; the remainder stays with the owner and cannot be paid twice by rounding. Each distribution binds a unique canonical withdraw journal and exact cash delta. Fresh source checks reconcile prior amounts, original partner credits, cumulative ratio, and metadata counters against actual canonical evidence. Metadata alone cannot invent unpaid profit or funding. Owner/partner wallet capacity is checked, no FX or fee is introduced, and paired ledger sums are zero.

Fresh character/account, depot ownership/status, latest policy, agreement/hash, database deadline, bounded source rows and payment proof are reread under canonical characters/accounts→depot→policy→agreement locks. Current owner agent actions use the exact existing `guardDelegatedControl`; normal agent/human permissions remain unchanged. Acceptance and contributions are separate fresh explicit actions by original participants, never a foreign-owner flag. Request receipts bind original account and exact normalized body/key, with conditional state/counter updates in the same native transaction.

## Death and privacy

Owner death keeps existing canonical depot closure/burn. Partner death freezes new contributions/distributions while remaining at-risk capital stays ordinary canonical depot capital; there is no new counterpart cash write or extra burn. The retirement helper verifies the victim is already dead, then updates only agreement metadata. The component test invokes it after actual `runEstate`; shared estate wiring is deliberately deferred to the coordinator. Even without that hook, fresh actions require both original living characters and an open original-owner depot; automatic heirs gain no fresh rights. Original accounts retain private historical board/replay acknowledgments. Closing metadata refunds nothing and preserves canonical owner withdrawal control.

Only the two original accounts can read an agreement/quote. Agreed capital/profit amounts are shared privately; customer identities, source journal IDs, other parties' balances and wallets are not returned. No automatic payout, vote, governance, owner configuration proxy, or source price override exists. Future F14 control rights require separate authorization.
