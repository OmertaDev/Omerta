# Merchant-funded sale attribution

An original merchant can reserve a fixed game-cash commission for one active consignment of an existing physical tool. A named original publisher must accept the exact terms. A different original buyer records immutable attribution before buying through that campaign. Completion transfers the real item and pays the original consignment's buyer, merchant and storefront-host cash legs; a separate ledgered payment releases the merchant's previously funded commission to the publisher.

There are no traffic rewards, synthetic purchases, points, cash-out balances or external messages. All four original accounts must be distinct, active and alive at purchase. Human and agent players use the same authenticated routes. An ordinary consignment purchase remains available and earns no advertising commission. Attribution cannot be added after that sale.

Each merchant can have three funded campaigns. A listing can have only one campaign, and a physical UID can earn a commission only once across resales. A buyer can hold three unexpired active attributions. Incoming campaign offers create no publisher spending authority, custody or estate counterpart scope. A campaign expires at the earlier of its own deadline and the original listing deadline. The merchant can cancel or expire it and recover its own unused reserve. The publisher earns no liability before the actual atomic sale completes.

The commission is bounded by the merchant's original net listing proceeds and by actual net seller proceeds at completion. An optional loyalty discount changes the buyer's payment and seller proceeds, while the original storefront host fee remains intact. Distinct accounts do not prove independent human ownership; colluding accounts receive no minted subsidy because every commission consumes the merchant's prior reserve.

Fresh purchase actions resolve and lock exactly the four original characters and their accounts in sorted order before campaign, attribution, listing or physical-item locks. The private context is created only inside that action. A genuine new consignment mutation must mint a same-transaction proof containing exact item, original parties, three journal IDs, guard and release/transfer events. A historical receipt or caller-shaped token cannot create a commission. An exact same-account, same-key retry returns its prior receipt without producing value.

Merchant death refunds only the merchant's own reserve before canonical estate cash settlement, using genuine private estate and rental scopes. It adds the refund delta to the unsaved victim cash rather than replacing other earned cash. Publisher or buyer death cannot give their replacement character the original's right to purchase or collect the commission. Ordinary consignment title recovery remains canonical.

The component intentionally fails closed until the canonical listing, contextual purchase and fresh-proof leaves are installed. Shared schema, registry, gateway, accounting, estate and release wiring belong to coordinator integration. Component memory and HTTP proofs are not PostgreSQL locking, native estate rollback, source-pair, deployment or financial-activation clearance. Live financial rails remain disabled.
