# ADR 003 — Original claims and provenance remain immutable

Accepted and implemented for Coordination Phase 01 review, 2026-09-13. The original Phase 00 review remains historical evidence for its earlier scope.

Decision: a discovered claim retains its original source receipt and origin root. Sharing changes access, and corroboration/contradiction add relations. Neither overwrites the original proposition. Derived confidence/status is recomputable from authorized, qualified evidence; a player assertion alone is not truth. Copies preserve origin and cannot create independent corroboration.

Consequences: contradictory history remains auditable, revocation affects future access, and archives can preserve institutional evidence without creating new discoveries. Secret links and provenance require the same non-enumerating access checks as the claim itself. Exact mechanics and acceptance tests belong to Phase 01.
