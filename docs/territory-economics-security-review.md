## Effect and authority

An original living Depot operator opts into sharing incremental, verified customer profit with the original consenting family while that family still controls the Depot district. A real paid F19 concession, paid premises occupancy, paid original-landlord tool rental and healthy physical tool remain mandatory. There is no price/demand multiplier, new money bucket, material mint, family-title succession, external spending consent, paid compute or financial activation.

The private authenticated component registrar exposes GET `/v1/company/territory-economics`, and POST `/configure`, `/run`, `/close`. Exact closed bodies and bounded IDs/request keys are required. Configuration limits: share 1–2000 basis points; daily cap $100–$5,000; retained lifetime cap $100–$10,000; TTL 60–3,600 seconds, additionally capped to F19 expiry. Revisions retain original actor/account, family, business, concession, baseline and paid totals. Explicit owner revision may renew a cap within the same absolute maximum; it never resets spent amounts. Agent configuration uses the established `guardDelegatedControl` against the actual business ID, so an existing restrictive policy requires the owner session. A consented bounded run is regular game automation, not permission to edit that policy or borrow its procurement budget. Current F19 policy snapshot and Depot reserve remain authoritative.

F19 context is resolved before G, then revalidated inside `G.withCharacter`'s trusted `withActionScope`. F19 sorts operator/landlord actors/accounts and both Depots, locks its real property/lease/rental sources, and keeps its private scope active through gameplay. F20 subsequently locks district, current family, membership, profile and source journals. F19 final validation ends before G persists the actor/account/kitchen; the Items owner commits once. No nested G/Items owner, production proxy pool, fabricated account owner, or generic authority registrar is used.

The closed product refuses any family with `war_with`, including an overdue war. Canonical tribute otherwise invokes a two-family war settlement that takes sorted family locks; this refusal avoids introducing an unsorted second-family acquisition. Ordinary family tribute emits existing synchronous UI events; a rolled-back operation may emit that inherited UI notification. No external customer messaging is implemented.

## Source and accounting

Execution reads a complete bounded window of at most 500 Depot journals and 1,000 relevant customer/market-fee transactions. Baseline IDs are exact UUID sets, not timestamps or lexicographic UUID chronology. Missing baseline rows, truncated windows, nonintegral arithmetic, mismatched customer cash/fee books or same-original-account fresh customers refuse payment as UNKNOWN. The independent oracle uses complete unbounded queries, so a valid larger world does not become a false conservation failure.

Only fresh canonical sale cash after the market take, minus canonical sale COGS and all new expense fields, contributes to incremental profit. Funding, refunds, rental/royalty income and other positive journals do not become customer income. Sale/customer aggregate cash witnesses reconcile with actual `depot:buy` and `depot:take` transactions. Existing business opening/history before consent is excluded by the explicit incremental baseline; the module does not claim lifetime business profitability.

Available share is cumulative earned share minus previously paid cash, limited by current day/lifetime consent and spendable Depot treasury after the canonical $1,000/current-policy reserve. Below canonical tribute minimum $100, no cash moves; earned residual remains unpaid. Exact globally unique sale-source references prevent registering one source twice; accumulated earned credit may be paid in multiple bounded draws without increasing its total. Each immutable payment records original source IDs, actual historical profit/rate/caps, prior paid amount, day and the actual paired withdrawal/tribute witnesses. Later unrelated business expenses or lower future consent caps do not retroactively invalidate a historically lawful payment.

Payment is exactly `Depot.withdrawDepot` followed by canonical `Gang.tribute` on the same actor and branded client: Depot treasury decreases, the actor's net cash change is zero, family treasury increases. Existing `depot:withdraw` / `gang:tribute` reasons and global cash/GIN/treasury books are used unchanged. No new balance bucket/reason is required. Expiry is refreshed after journal waits, after canonical ancillary locks, after tribute and at F19 final validation. A native rollback is the actual database rollback. For pg-mem only, registered compensation covers the exact newly inserted receipt/source/payment/transaction rows, new canonical withdrawal journal from the already locked Depot, Depot cash, affected family treasury/standing/weekly/reserve fields, daily counter and weekly event fund. This is not a generalized legacy SQL rollback claim.
