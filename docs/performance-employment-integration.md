# Performance employment

An original business employer may add a prefunded performance bonus to one of its genuine prepaid delivery jobs before any worker accepts. Existing wages, delivery terms, human/agent eligibility and ordinary employment permissions stay in force. The rider specifies a minimum completed quantity and a fixed bonus of at most 10,000 game cash per slot; the original one-to-five slots cap the total reserve at 50,000. The actual employer pays that reserve up front. There is no new balance, reward mint, demand or wage duplication.

Acceptance binds the worker's original identity and the exact current F12 assignment/commitment before any delivery. Each actual committed market fill records its genuine quantity, two original payout/take journal IDs, cumulative rounding, immutable source and completion time. A complete on-time commitment meeting the agreed quantity earns its bonus alongside its once-only original wage. A genuine below-threshold completion still earns its ordinary wage and draws no bonus. Completing a delivery before its deadline permits the normal later wage claim within the original job's claim window; claim time is not substituted for completion time.

The rider cannot be retrofitted to accepted workers or funded from a caller-reported score, arbitrary cash object, callback or claimed result. Funding locks the real original owner and debits actual SQL cash using the canonical ledger. The ordinary delegated-spending guard applies. Fixed private F12 and market leaves mint temporary opaque proofs bound to their actual client, Items transaction, action and exact locked context. Their exports verify existing proofs; they cannot mint or install a context. Missing canonical leaves make fresh rider funding fail closed. Ordinary jobs keep their original receipt shapes and settlement semantics.

Closing the original job returns its unused wage and bonus reserves through the original owner paths. Actual employer estate resolution burns/loots the remaining bonus with the same bounded estate rate, using the existing private runEstate capability and original in-memory killer cash. The rider introduces no additional original actor union. A successor can acknowledge old account-scoped receipts but cannot exercise the dead original's job authority. Workers cannot claim an unpaid outcome through a deceased employer's street.

The coordinator owns shared canonical schema, E/market leaves, server/gateway registration, monetary reason/source classification, invariants, graph/census, migration catalogs, CI and release integration. Three new tables retain contracts, exact assignment outcomes and authored funding receipts. Current reserve is reconciled to the actual reserve/bonus/refund/estate ledger. The owned route and closed contracts cover `/v1/company/performance-employment`; actual acceptance, delivery, wage claim and close remain the canonical existing endpoints.

The frozen off-repository prototype exercises genuine bought-gin commitments, partial fills and physical custody, actual wage and bonus cash, refused spoofed outcomes, normal JWT parity, original spending policy, caught/late rollback, ordinary employment regressions, canonical original estates and real elapsed deadlines. Its strict `--postgres` fixtures additionally specify full canonical-table/sequence rollback proofs, including the full underlying market mutation, but those native branches require separate coordinator qualification. pg-mem success does not prove PostgreSQL locks, races or full-market/full-world estate rollback. Final exact integrated source, security, migration, native and release checks remain required.

This feature spends game cash. Live payments, real paid compute and financial activation remain disabled. No external payout or financial backing is implied.
