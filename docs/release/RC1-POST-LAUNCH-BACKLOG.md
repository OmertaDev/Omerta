# RC1 nonblocking observations

These observations do not independently override release gates or justify new architecture.

- Opportunity density: measured boards can contain more than sixty cards. Decide pacing from completed first-session and cohort evidence; the count alone does not establish a severe UX failure.
- Dock War opportunity copy: existing generic readiness text can communicate more specific stakes. Keep this separate from the confirmed consequence and acquisition wording failures corrected during RC1.
- Broader catalog variety, additional districts, mysteries, recipes, NPCs, Family systems and animations remain expansion work.
- Long-term return rate and whether human players understand the effects of their actions require the controlled cohort. Automated browser completion does not establish enjoyment or retention.

Required incomplete gates and the solo progression dead end belong in RC1-READINESS.md, not in this backlog.
