# Bounded Law-pressure component

`createLawPolicy({accountId,seed})` consumes a verified bearer subject, ordinary `/v1/session`, `/v1/me` and `/v1/law` views plus the public `/v1/rules` catalog. It never reads database observer snapshots. `choose(view,{logicalAt,phase})` accepts recorded `pressure`, `plea`, `trial`, `await-sweep` and `recover` phases. Pressure selects the cheapest publicly eligible nerve-cost crime with the published loud approach, seeded within ties. At heat 95 or higher it observes a 300-second window; ordinary nerve shortages and detention return their actual waits. An already indicted actor can persist the observed case through another eligible pressure crime before the caller switches phase. These are workload choices, not a claim that other legal choices are unavailable.

Plea and trial require the own public indictment. Recovery selects the quiet zero-heat approach after detention and any indictment clear. Trial RNG is never pinned. Unimplemented bribe/retainer/jury/envelope/informant/protection and paid cooling branches remain explicit. This component fails closed on a different character identity; it does not implement heir rotation.

Persist `checkpoint()` before dispatching the exact request. `settle({idempotencyKey,status,replayed,response})` verifies completed or denied results against that identity; known exact replay does not count fresh progress. Missing responses keep pending method/path/body/key. A completed replay whose original result is unknown retains its full decision and receipt through restart and blocks new choices for caller reconciliation. Unit tests cover public level/nerve/heat/indictment gates, original detention waits, unissued/foreign identities, hidden-input tripwires, receipt conflicts and deterministic restart.

The native exercise uses one ordinary guest and character with original numeric starting resources and zero direct fixtures. Canonical loud pickpocketing builds actual heat, watched/investigation stages and indictment within a predeclared 24-hour logical bound. The case is persisted by an ordinary action, then a canonical plea must transfer exactly its quoted 15% cash+bank loss to the confiscation pool and ledger. Exact plea retry must leave the full state unchanged. A crime attempted while jailed must be canonically denied, one second before the original 240-second deadline the actor must still be detained, and after that deadline a quiet crime must resume actual cash/respect progression on the same living character.
