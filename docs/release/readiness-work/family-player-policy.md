# Bounded Family membership and leadership component

`createFamilyPolicy({accountId,seed,scenario,population:25})` uses a verified bearer subject, ordinary own session/character views, the public Family directory and detail roster, and public rules. It selects canonical formation, immediate joining, cash tribute, boss promotion, departure and quiet personal progression. `choose(view,{logicalAt,phase,targetFamilyId})` records intended phases; an optional target must exist in the current public directory. Monopoly entry prefers the largest Family. Fragmented entry prefers the smallest Family below the declared five-member workload ceiling. A canonical full denial is remembered at the observed member count; a public vacancy permits another fresh choice. These observations never prove the absence of all legal progression.

Persist the complete pending request through `checkpoint()` before dispatch, then `settle({idempotencyKey,status,replayed,response})`. Known exact receipt replays add no fresh completion. Missing responses preserve their key; an unknown completed replay retains its full decision/receipt and blocks further choices for caller reconciliation. Foreign account/character views, wrong leader rosters, unissued public targets and changed replay receipts fail closed. This component uses legacy canonical endpoints; it does not invent PlayerCommand authority or a Family request/approval queue.

Source review of `src/social/gangs.js` and `src/rules.tail.js` establishes level 5 and $25,000 for formation and 20 members maximum. Joining has no level/cash requirement and takes effect immediately. Only the boss promotes; boss/underboss may kick. Departure hands leadership to an underboss, then the existing rank/order rule. The component exercises promotion/succession, not kicks, alliance, war or operations.

`planFamilyFixture` declares exactly 25 ordinary accounts/characters. Monopoly starts with one Family of 20 and five ordinary outsiders: exact 80%. Fragmentation starts with ten Families (three of size 3, seven of size 2) and two ordinary outsiders: no Family exceeds the 20% ceiling. Only founder respect receives an initialization fixture, to legal level 75, explicitly not earned. Each founder then receives an actual ordinary $26,250 check-in and pays the actual $25,000 formation cost. All memberships and concentration use canonical joins/tribute. There are no direct cash, item, membership or privilege grants. Exact progression writes and all initialization receipts precede the measured boundary.

All initial members contribute their available cash, leaving monopoly treasury $11,250 versus $500 per outsider, or $24,000 spread over the ten fragmented Families. Largest means the collective cash position within this finite cohort for resources actually exercised. No global maximum or all-resource dominance is claimed. Full snapshots and the initial distribution retain other balances and ownership; their unexercised dimensions stay unqualified.

Every declared outsider attempts canonical entry and ordinary quiet crime progression from unmodified starting resources. Monopoly outsiders receive actual full denials; after boss departure and canonical succession one outsider takes the public vacancy. Fragmented outsiders join small Families without exceeding the declared ceiling. Exact join/tribute retries must preserve the full state, and treasury custody must survive leadership succession. The remaining initial members mostly participate during initialization, so these cases do not demonstrate 25 active actors throughout a 90-day workload.
