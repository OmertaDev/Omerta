# Commercial campaign source interface

This N20 component begins at `99edbb31928d3cafaf574be320b4b895f37b6f44`. It depends on actual commissioned crafting, multi-hop courier networks and consenting management teams. This is a bounded implementation design; it is not a deployed feature or release qualification.

The campaign has four dependent stages: a paid public-market scout report, commissioned manufacturing, purchased guard coverage and delivery of the manufactured item through the existing two-leg courier network. Its owner is the actual team operator and its scout is the actual team planner. Four additional, distinct original accounts are the manufacturer, guard and two carriers. The actual original manufacturer owns the referenced business. Every original consents to the fixed terms before activation.

Only all-consented active memberships occupy capacity. One original can have one active campaign. An owner can have one offered or active proposal. An unsigned foreign proposal cannot reserve another player's membership. Activation creates the six active memberships atomically, in canonical account order. Suspension is terminal and releases metadata memberships. Campaign recovery changes metadata only; existing source components retain their own cash, item and estate obligations.

| Stage | Actual canonical source and business reference | Completion evidence and authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scout | `intel_forecast_jobs`, original owner as buyer and actual planner as reporter; original district, fee, minimum stock and exact original target | Actual reserve/pay journals and the original timely server-captured public market snapshot. A prediction is not a profitable outcome or a manufacturing demand guarantee. |
| Manufacture | `commissioned_crafting_contracts.business_id` references the manufacturer's original `business_depots.id`; original owner supplies the recipe inputs and fixed fee | Actual accepted precision-lock-tool commission, original recipe/input mutation witnesses, canonical production receipt, paid fee and resulting customer-owned UID. This proves purchased protection and its window. It never claims an attack occurred or a bullet was intercepted. |
| Courier | `multi_hop_couriers`, original owner, two original carriers, same manufactured UID and three distinct districts | Existing actual owner-funded escrow, both independent carrier consents, real travel journals, release/re-escrow handoff, original-owner final custody and both earned fees. |

Fees are independently reserved or paid by the owner at each gated canonical stage. The campaign has no money bucket, asset mint, additional reward, generic actor list, extra Game or rental arity, delegated owner spending grant or payment currency. Earlier earned fees remain earned if a later stage fails. Existing source refund and estate paths remain available.

Campaigns last 60–3600 seconds. The original `scoutTargetAt` pins the exact forecast target. Funding must pass the existing genuine 5–30-minute lead and price-block rules at the time of intake. The scout stage pays for a timely report captured now. Its later accuracy outcome can outlive the campaign and is not a prerequisite or a claimed result. Scout actions retain their source's mobile-state checks and Game's ordinary meeting behavior. Guard hire retains `requireKnown:true`. Game's `meet:true` records a completed meeting; it does not enforce equal locations. The known-player check applies when the target is elsewhere or meeting is disabled, so an already known remote guard remains eligible under the existing rules.

The actual bodyguard service requires an unexpired guard assignment, an available living guard and a guard who is not the attacker. The atomic absorption consumes the assignment after one successful save. Neither hiring nor absorption adds a location condition. Jailhouse shanking bypasses street bodyguards. Purchased coverage is therefore evidence of the actual service and window, not a guarantee against every attack or prison risk.

The runtime must resolve the actual team relationship hash/revision, all six current original characters and active accounts, business ownership and current policy. Stored request bodies, caller-supplied rows and pure term validators grant no authority. Source references use typed foreign keys, unique source consumption and closed receipt reconstruction. Requests and source hashes bind the actual account, original character, action, source and exact terms.

The metadata activation step does not promise atomic liveness of six characters. A provider death can race the final signer's check and briefly leave an inert active membership. Every actual task still locks and verifies its genuine canonical originals before mutation and after final ACK, so that row grants no execution for a dead or replaced original. A new enrollment checks only the affected account's single indexed active membership and retires genuinely expired or dead-original metadata. This includes an heir clearing an old inert membership, without executing an old task or spending any old balance. Completed historical stage sources remain immutable. No six-character prelocker is added.

Each canonical source needs a fixed private fresh completion witness. A literal source call mints it only after its genuine mutation; it is bound to the actual client and Items transaction, consumed once, and revalidated after the source's final receipt/account ACK and real deadline. Replayed requests, old unrelated tasks, caller-created capability objects and mutated source projections cannot advance a campaign. Shared source changes are off-repository proposals for coordinator review, never alternate payment or actor adapters.

Ordinary canonical actions remain independently executable, including already funded obligations after campaign retirement. Completing a source through its ordinary route does not fabricate a campaign ACK or relabel that old result. The invited-participant board reports the current supported stage, a concrete `canonical_source_completed_independently` or changed-source blocker, and the original owner's cancel or signed provider's withdraw path. Retirement changes no source payout, item, refund or earned fee. HTTP actions use the same authenticated human or agent account and actual canonical stage authority.

The scout and guard retain actual two-original Game roots. Commissioned crafting retains its actual original customer/manufacturer Items transaction. Courier actions retain their source-resolved three-original courier root. Campaign metadata adds no counterparty cash writes or death prelock actors. The exact metadata estate bound is one active membership plus at most one own unsigned proposal, with excess treated as an error rather than truncated.

Financial activation, real compute purchases and crypto settlement remain disabled. OMR hire-fee utility is a separate design and does not change these cash-denominated gameplay stages.
