# Consensual default auctions for pledged equipment

An original borrower can propose default-auction terms for an existing directed cash-loan offer and an existing standard crafted precision-tool UID. The original lender approves that exact source before the borrower takes the loan through ordinary equipment collateral. These signatures bind the UID, original birth/condition, quote, full debt, reserve, duration and proceeds allocation. Taking first and signing later never creates a sale right. Unsigned proposals reserve no item, money, loan slot or counterpart locks.

The existing loan holder may open the auction only after the genuine ordinary due time. The right follows lawful paper ownership until an auction opens. A later paper transfer cancels that live auction and refunds original bidders before the ordinary purchase commits. Original terms do not add an ordinary paper-ownership cap.

Up to two other original players can submit sealed, genuinely cash-funded bids. Each original can have one outstanding auction relationship. The highest cash bid wins; server bid ID breaks ties. Bids below the reserve are refused, and the reserve must cover the actual full debt. Bidders authorize escrowed money, never a later extra debit. Bid amounts remain private until closing. The original borrower or current holder can cancel an empty auction before any bid; this does not cancel the ordinary debt or release its pledge.

Settlement consumes the winning escrow, credits the original borrower, executes the actual ordinary loan repayment and vig, releases the original N23 escrow to its depositor, and then transfers that same UID to the winning account. The borrower keeps the excess. Losing bids refund immediately. No lender title, fictional cash, unsupported mint, free spending authority or new actor arity is introduced.

Original death, live claim transfer, genuine ordinary debt repayment/collection or expiry of the one-hour settlement window cancels the auction and returns held cash to its original characters. Estate refunds occur before the ordinary estate cash snapshot; they follow existing death rules and never become a new heir grant. The equipment follows ordinary N23 custody and estate rules unless an actual consensual paid sale completed.

Human and agent clients share closed authenticated endpoints under `/v1/loans/equipment/auctions`. Positive mutation requires native PostgreSQL and fixed canonical consent, loan, custody, preactor, estate, accounting and finalization hooks. The coordinator owns all shared schema/hooks/catalogs and integration; their proposal is separate. This component keeps financial activation, crypto settlement, live payments and paid compute disabled. A source-pinned naturally elapsed loan proof is required for positive default-auction release; a backdated due date is not a substitute.
