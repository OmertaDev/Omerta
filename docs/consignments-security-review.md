## Model and interfaces

`src/consignments.js` composes existing `withItemTransaction`, `withItemMutation(operation_action)`, `escrowItem`, `releaseEscrow`, `transferItem`, physical-condition validation and `G.ledger`. No raw physical-item writes, asset creation, synthetic demand, free spending authority, escrow cash bucket or generic three-party framework is introduced.

Exports:

- `createConsignment(client, accountId, body, requestKey)` records an original owner's exact quote. The supported UID remains active and account-owned until the original host accepts.
- `acceptConsignment`, `buyConsignment`, `cancelConsignment`, `expireConsignment` take `{consignmentId, sourceHash, consent:true}`. Requests bind account/key/body/action before fresh eligibility. Completed receipts are historical acknowledgments; they never reauthorize heirs.
- `consignmentBoard(client, accountId, after)` returns at most25 rows; only original owner/host see private item and original-party IDs. Public quotes contain no UID, account ID, balance or private buyer identity. Availability is an informational expiry check; execution revalidates current originals and custody.
- `lockConsignmentParties` locks up to three original characters in sorted order, then their accounts. Purchase requires three distinct accounts, all living originals, active accounts and buyer pickup at the current host district. Normal signed human/agent participation is symmetric; no separate owner-session permission is created.
- `consignmentDeathCounterparts`, `prelockConsignmentDeath`, `recoverConsignmentsAtDeath` provide a bounded component recovery contract. See the mandatory shared integration limitation below.
- `collectConsignmentIssues` reconciles live escrow and sold rows against exactly three canonical cash legs and their receipt IDs. It is a new component check, not a replacement for existing global books.

Closed terms: one existing standard-provenance precision-tool UID; current physical usability means remaining condition>=10, not a promise of pristine quality. Price1..1,000,000 game cash, host fee0..2,000 basis points, server-clock lifetime60..86,400 seconds. Fee=floor(price*bps/10000); owner receives price-fee. The buyer pays price, and the three journal amounts sum exactly to zero, including a zero fee. No cash is paid for an unaccepted or cancelled offer.

Owner capacity is eight offered+active quotes; host capacity is sixteen **accepted active** consignments. A live quote is unique per owner/UID, and accepted custody is globally unique per UID. Host acceptance rechecks the original owner's current healthy title before escrow. Original beneficial title is retained by the existing `operation_escrow.depositor_scope/depositor_id`; the host receives neither title nor tool-use authority. Settlement releases only to that depositor and then transfers to the consenting third-party buyer through canonical Items leaves.
