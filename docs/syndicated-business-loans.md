# Syndicated business loans — canonical component

This bounded component lets an original borrower invite two distinct original lenders to fund equal unsecured loans from their own character cash. It executes ordinary Loans offers, take and repayment through a fixed canonical three-original G/R dispatch. Principal per lender is 5000..500000 game cash; total is at most the existing 1000000 loan maximum. Both lenders sign the exact immutable terms before the borrower separately takes the pair. One current consenting facility is permitted per account. Naming a lender grants no membership or spending permission.

The two physical canonical loan rows represent one verified facility only inside the dedicated private take. Ordinary MAX_ACTIVE remains one. Independent take, partial repayment or paper transfer cannot split an intact facility; a third/house debt cannot enter. Full repayment pays both original canonical amounts, including the unchanged ordinary vig. No pooled money balance, cash reason, credit, collateral, customer-principal capital or outside payment is introduced. Default collection remains ordinary individually authorized recovery: outcomes can be unequal or zero.

Only a borrower with zero funded legs can cancel a proposal. A funded lender may refund only its own open canonical offer, which retires paired admission. The other lender's escrow and ordinary refund rights remain intact. Missing or retired legs can never be taken independently. Actual expiration retires membership without moving funds; each original lender retains ordinary refund recovery.

The fixed dispatcher resolves and revalidates exactly three actual originals through a dedicated private R scope, then uses existing G load/accrue/persistence for all three characters, owned state and accounts. It has no callback installer, supplied helper factory or ambient pool capability. The generic two-original admission, existing 57-per-subject death family and courier capability are unchanged. After actual request writes and all three G persistence writes, source, active original bindings and database time are checked again before completion.

Canonical ordinary loans remain the only money authority. Four new tables hold immutable source metadata, actual original funding journal/receipt links, current consenting membership and account/key/action/body-bound receipts. No new Depot/business foreign key exists. Canonical loan IDs intentionally have no foreign key to volatile loan rows, preserving honest disappearance after estate resolution. Read-only provenance checks join actual proposal/funding consent, original parties, canonical loans and cash journals; they introduce no second cash book.

The estate hook runs after actual ordinary loan death resolution and only retires consent metadata. Genuine unsecured terms bind collateral to zero. Consequently it writes no surviving counterpart cash/account balance: original open lender escrow burns/loots by existing rules; dead-borrower active claims disappear; active lender claims pass to the actual heir. An heir retains ordinary current-holder recovery but receives no old facility authority. At most one current membership/facility, up to two actually funded lenders and one borrower are relevant. This observation does not replace native lock/concurrency qualification.

With immutable ordinary shared roots, every fresh action remains unavailable, even if a caller supplies a counterfeit capability. The separate historical scaffold review records its older phase and must not be read as canonical integration evidence.
