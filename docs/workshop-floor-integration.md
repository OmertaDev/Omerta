# Workshop floor planning

Owners may allocate the two existing commercial-premises slots of an established estate or matching offchain street property among a quality tool bench, storage and a service bay. A plan restricts genuine business admission; it provides no production bonus, new recipe authority, item, cash, demand, storage units or payment rail.

A tool bench binds the exact healthy account-owned precision-tool UID already admitted by the business's existing quality specialization. The three supported authored recipes remain unchanged: hardened steel, precision lock tool and tempered precision lock tool. None of those recipes has been rewritten to require a fictional machine. Quality admission continues to verify that exact tool through the existing specialization policy; rented, escrowed, sold, consumed or damaged tools do not qualify as the owner's bench. One physical UID cannot serve two genuine simultaneous benches. Recipe inputs, output definitions, cash costs, grant budgets, wear and cadence remain canonical.

Nonquality F01 production and ordinary funded N03/N09 work require an allocated service bay. Existing quality restrictions on N03/N09 remain; a service bay does not override them. New commission funding is refused before material custody or cash movement if no service bay is available. Removing service is refused while real funded contracts or offered/active reservations remain. Their original deadlines, customer identities, source hashes, fees and physical outputs are preserved.

Storage enables new depot procurement and new warehouse offers/leases. It retains the original 40-unit gin limit; commercial slots are not converted into invented storage units. Plans cannot remove storage while real stock, procurement promises or reserved/held warehouse cargo exist. Receiving existing funded deliveries, retrieving paid tenant cargo, closing claims, withdrawing stock and recovering existing contracts retain their original recovery paths.

Real paid premises leases reserve their exact slot until expiry or canonical closure. Owners cannot allocate those slots, and future lease acceptance refuses an owner-allocated slot before rent moves. Unaccepted offers and cancelled/expired history confer no permanent blocking authority. The current node supports owner-authored plans; it grants no tenant-authored plan or free landlord consent.

## Integration contract

The owned component fails closed on fresh configuration until all five actual fixed admission/ingress families exist: F01, depot, warehouse, premises lease and N03 funding. Each canonical leaf privately mints a short-lived opaque context after its existing authority/source checks. Only verifiers are exported. Contexts bind the same branded Items client and transaction, exact internal purpose/context object and actual canonical entrypoint. There is no public context factory, callback installer, caller pricing resolver or ambient pool authority.

The coordinator owns the canonical DDL, registrations, gateway action/closed response schemas, source taxonomy, source pins, native/migration catalogs, test/CI wiring, census, graph and release. Exact off-repository proposals compose those fixed private leaves and public registrar on the pinned base, with no generic G/R grant or new estate counterpart root. The two proposed tables hold current source-bound configuration and retained request/history only; they hold no cash or inventory.

Fresh configuration locks the current original owner/account, existing business, company profile, genuine estate/deed backing and live paid occupancy before replacing metadata; selected bench authority is revalidated against actual quality policy and custody. Source-bound admission repeats the relevant source checks while canonical original locks are held. Historical account/key/body acknowledgments return their old receipt; they do not grant a successor authority over an old business or old source. Canonical owner death continues to close the original business and recover funded obligations through existing private estate bridges. The floor plan/history itself introduces no cash DELTA or estate cleanup liability.

The source-frozen prototype proves real canonical production, funded N09 completion, normal JWT parity, exact replay, paid tenant/stock/warehouse protection, actual procurement and bought-gin flow, source/receipt/opaque-scope adversarial refusal, caught/late rollback, actual sorted-original estates and real elapsed lease expiry. Native test guards require an empty named local/CI database and include full-table/allocator assertions, but memory does not qualify PostgreSQL races, lock ordering or all-world estate rollback. Root must review and qualify the actual final integrated revision before release. Financial activation and paid compute remain disabled.
