## Joint custody and estate findings

Order/fee journal legs have NULL character and cooperative counterparty, never an organizer pocket debit. Canonical fill pays from existing order escrow and holds delivered GIN in the canonical order until joint settlement atomically zeros source qty/filled_qty, captures custody/refund and allocates integer rights. Personal Market claim and cancel reject any bound order before payout; sweep detects the binding and calls the sole joint settler before personal refund logic. Founder death cannot route pooled funds or GIN through a personal Market refund. Both generic estate selection and final DELETE exclude every bound order; living members retain the canonical binding and rights.

runEstate calls joint recovery before generic Market transition, then canonical loan/favor/forecast/payroll dispositions before heir insertion. It passes only its caller-approved clamped <=0.5 player-kill rate and original in-memory killer. Joint recovery takes all canonical order locks before cooperative/member locks, checks source_changed after acquisition, settles funding/live custody and removes only the deceased original member's unclaimed entitlement. Existing already-claimed personal holdings remain generic estate assets. Loot journals pair a killer pocket credit with joint custody debit; unlooted cash and unclaimed GIN burn with explicit journals. No cash/GIN rights go to the heir and duplicate death is inert. Caller trust of the pure recovery helper is explicit: it is not a public killer/rate-authority API.

Payroll reserve is debited from original employer cash, once-only canonical delivery assignment claims transfer backed wage, and estate closes unused reserve with paired approved killer loot/rest burn. NPC killers are accepted only as canonical estate actors, not employment enrollment parties. Original killer persistence is delegated to the existing game transaction wrapper; direct SQL killer cash writes would be clobbered and are avoided. Global named employment escrow/loot checks and joint cash/GIN/provenance checks reconcile buckets against journal identities.
