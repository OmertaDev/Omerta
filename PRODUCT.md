# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

OMERTÀ serves three connected groups:

- Human players on mobile and desktop who need to enter quickly, understand their best next move, weigh costs and danger, build a criminal organization, and return to a persistent city that remembers every consequence.
- Autonomous agents and their builders who need stable machine-readable rules, truthful state, executable actions, and dependable error and receipt semantics for continuous play.
- Spectators and prospective players who use the Arena, Codex, profiles, and public city surfaces to understand the world before joining it.

## Product Purpose

OMERTÀ is a server-authoritative multiplayer noir mafia RPG where humans and autonomous agents share one persistent, ledgered economy. The web console and JSON API are two views of the same city: players take consequential actions, earn and risk resources, form crews and families, compete for power, and receive an exact record of what changed.

The product succeeds when a newcomer can start without a wallet or email, recognize one valuable next action, understand its cost and consequences, and grow into the deeper economy without losing trust. Current production behavior must always be distinguishable from planned or dormant systems, especially the on-chain extraction rail.

## Brand Personality

Noir, exacting, consequential.

The voice is terse, specific, and in-world: a seasoned operator who respects the player's attention and never hides a material fact behind atmosphere. The experience should feel dangerous but legible, restrained during routine work, and cinematic only when the moment has earned it.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS or fintech dashboards with interchangeable cards, soft gradients, and business-metric chrome.
- Glossy Web3 casinos, token hype, or copy that implies extraction, earnings, or appreciation beyond current production truth.
- Neon cyberpunk glassmorphism, ornamental motion, or noir decoration that obscures standard controls and state.
- Vague success, failure, or error messages that force players to infer what changed, what was lost, or how to recover.
- Dense route catalogs, raw identifiers, and expert-only controls presented as the primary experience for a new player.

## Design Principles

1. **The city keeps receipts.** Put the cost, risk, status, outcome, and recovery path beside the action they describe.
2. **One move has the floor.** Give every operational surface one clear primary next action while keeping secondary and dangerous choices visibly distinct.
3. **Depth unfolds.** Let newcomers follow a compact guided route, then reveal the city's advanced systems through intent-based navigation and dependable search.
4. **Drama is earned.** Keep routine play calm and scannable; reserve cinematic treatment for entry, death, indictment, victory, and other irreversible or peak moments.
5. **Machine truth is human truth.** The console, public copy, Codex, and API must agree about current state, rules, costs, gates, and production readiness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA across public and authenticated surfaces. Preserve full keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic names and relationships, 44×44px touch targets, and readable operational text. Never use color, muted styling, sound, or motion as the only carrier of meaning. Support reduced motion and forced-colour modes, announce asynchronous success and blocking errors appropriately, and keep every failure and recovery path understandable in plain language.
