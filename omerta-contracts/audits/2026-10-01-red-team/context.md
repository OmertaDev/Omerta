## Cross-contract properties to establish

- Custody: native/OMR balances cover vested inventory notes, fee credits, reward liabilities, refunds, gas credits and LP withdrawal obligations. Governance retire/sweep/recover must exclude those liabilities.
- State ordering: observation epoch finalized and fresh; no snapshot reuse beyond per-action/per-episode allowances. Game mutations checkpoint fees using prior recipients; vault pause/recovery cannot retroactively earn rewards.
- Callback identity: callback must be reached from a live outer operation with matching PoolManager, currency, pool, action and budgets; nested recipient/token/NFT calls cannot replay it.
- Domain separation: voucher, outflow, successor consent, nonce, receipt and solver plans bind intended chain, contract, actor, asset and generation. Permissionless settlement/claim helpers pay immutable beneficiaries.
- Genesis atomicity: cancellation never accepts player funds; success never permits refunded accepted funds; position creation and release flag revert together on failed migration; public claims do not permanently depend on player settlement liveness.

## Assumptions without an on-chain truth verifier

These are trust assumptions, not confirmed defects.

1. Merkle eligibility corresponds to actual qualifying gameplay and correct day counts: nothing found on chain beyond root membership (GenesisPlayerSale.sol:83-101). Root creation and Sybil handling are off-chain.
2. Adjudicator turf ownership/status/siege outcome corresponds to game truth: nothing found on chain beyond authorized caller, receipt/revision/epoch guards (OmertaGameSettlementV2.sol:28,90-137; OmertaTurfV2.sol:261). The chain enforces attribution, not adjudication correctness.
3. Safe-curated stock identity, ballot tally and health clearance evidence correspond to real issuer/legal/off-chain state: nothing found on chain beyond publisher/Safe identity, payload hashes, registry and freshness checks (StockTokenRegistryV2.sol:46,131; RwaHealthOverlay.sol:45,89).
4. A runtime codehash proves dependency behavior/storage unchanged: nothing found for proxy implementation/storage upgrades from codehash alone (OmertaGenesisCoordinatorV2.sol:152-158; GenesisPlayerSale.sol:88,119). Determine whether configured dependencies are proxies and separately pin implementation/storage authority.
5. Market minimum active liquidity proves executable depth or economically honest directional volume: deliberately not established; source explicitly identifies virtual depth/flow as bounded signals (OmertaMarketStateV2.sol:9-12,49-61). Consumers must preserve this weaker meaning.
6. Reward position endpoint samples establish minimum useful depth continuously between observations: nothing found inside _checkpoint beyond endpoint min and max-gap checks (OmertaCommitmentVaultV2.sol:230-265). Position-manager ownership/call permissions must establish whether intermediate liquidity can change while custody is locked.
7. External ERC4626 valuations imply immediately realizable collateral: nothing found in the sampled collateralOf/maxDebtOf/totalAssets boundaries (Alchemist.sol:249-256; CollateralEscrow.sol:99). Follow deployment-selected vault implementation and withdrawal limits.
