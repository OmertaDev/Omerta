## Findings and dispositions

### RT-M02 / historical M-02 — medium, conditional unsupported oracle price

Affected path: `OmertaHook` → `OmrV4TwapOracle`, when an initialized pool has zero active liquidity. Initialization does not require liquidity; a zero-delta swap can change its tick; the hook accrues that tick; the oracle later publishes the resulting interval as fresh.

The executed `ComprehensiveMarketAudit.test_risk_emptyLiquidityProducesZeroCostFreshTwap` moves square-root price from Q96 to 2×Q96 without paying either asset and observes approximately 4× the original OMR/ETH quote. This establishes unsupported-price publication. It does **not** prove theft from a deployed bond: an economic-loss path additionally needs a consumer trusting that feed, absent liquidity and issuance/authorization conditions that permit use of the quote.

Disposition: **open, conditional; no clearance for consumers of this oracle under absent liquidity**. This historical finding was preserved rather than rewritten as a newly discovered exploit. The current market's observation/state path rejects zero-liquidity epochs; this does not clear other consumers. Remediation for any activation of the affected feed must validate liquidity across the entire observation interval, or use an independently depth-qualified feed and retest the consumer. A spot-only liquidity check would not establish that interval property.

### RT-P01 — acquisition funding/activation blocker

`AcquisitionVault`/`AcquisitionVaultCore` accept custody/accounting ingress but lack a complete corresponding asset release path. The authority's unpause fails closed and intent execution/reconciliation contain staged surfaces. Slither's two locked-ether diagnostics are accurate implementation/phase limitations, not false positives.

Disposition: **do not fund or activate these rails as a functioning acquisition system**. Existing local guards do not supply a withdrawal facility. No privileged or public theft was demonstrated. Completing execution/outflow changes reopens review.

### Configuration and operational residuals

- Immutable genesis-splitter recipients must accept ETH. One permanently rejecting recipient blocks the atomic distribution. No attacker-controlled redirection was established. ETH held before pool initialization is permissionlessly recoverable to the fixed treasury; pre-migration funding order must match this intended behavior.
- Player-sale settlement is permissionless and per-wallet; all participants must settle before proceeds release. Eligibility cardinality, keeper enumeration and deadline capacity need launch-specific rehearsal. Cancellation preserves the tested refund path; unavoidable/profitable settlement DoS was not demonstrated.
- Codehash pinning verifies runtime identity, not proxy implementation/storage immutability or semantic correctness. Actual manager, Permit2, auction, hook, Safe and asset selections require verification.
- Standard-token and selected ERC4626 valuation/withdrawal behavior are external assumptions. Fee-on-transfer, rebasing, callbacks and upgrades need configuration-specific validation.
- Turf adjudication, signer decisions, health feeds and governance remain explicit trusted authorities. No privilege-escalation path was confirmed; correctness of off-chain truth was outside scope.
- Cancun transient-storage support is a deployment prerequisite for the vendored guard.

These conditions are not silently accepted on the owner's behalf and prevent a universal deployment-ready conclusion.

1. **StreetDeed immutable district:** preserve original name/district through burn and remint; a fresh signed voucher changing district now reverts. This is signer-dependent integrity, not unprivileged theft. Retain the pre-fix failing proof (`evidence/deed-before-fix.txt` and its exit record), original source/test snapshots, and focused regression output. Do not overwrite those with post-fix logs.
2. **Oracle maximum periods:** both oracle constructors reject periods above `type(uint32).max / MAX_WINDOW_MULT` (1,073,741,823 seconds), preventing accepted parameters from overflowing the update window multiplier. This is an extreme trusted-configuration liveness boundary; ordinary configured periods retain their semantics.
3. **Vendored CCA fee quote:** initialization parameter reads clamp protocol fees to the raise, matching existing sweep behavior. An excessive nonzero fee controller previously caused arithmetic panic; current guarded auction fixes its controller to zero. This remedy restores quote/sweep consistency and cannot supply liquidity capital when net proceeds are zero. Original vendor pins/upstream hashes remain provenance; manifest/README explicitly record the local patch. Focused generic-auction regressions cover excessive fee and zero-controller behavior.
4. **Full wall-time Hook/oracle baselines:** Hook accumulation records full last-write time while preserving wrapped external timestamp/cumulative ABI; both oracle baselines track full wall time to discard an interval spanning a uint32 timestamp cycle instead of treating its wrapped residue as fresh. Long-window rebasing precedes short-window predicates. This is distinct from the retained unsupported empty-liquidity price condition; the latter remains open.
