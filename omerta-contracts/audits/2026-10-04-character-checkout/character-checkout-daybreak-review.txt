1. **Medium — payment can be duplicated after broadcast-response loss**

   [public/index.html:5649](C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/public/index.html:5649)

   Sequence: wallet broadcasts `eth_sendTransaction` → page closes/provider response is lost before line 5650 stores the hash → refresh finds no pending key → checkout offers another payment. OmertaFees accepts both, producing two paid credits.

   Correction: persist a pre-broadcast intent marker and require reconciliation or explicit cancellation before another payment; replace it with the hash when available. Backend pending-payment visibility would be stronger.

3. **Low — signer rotation permanently strands an expired voucher through this endpoint**

   [src/identity-checkout.js:105](C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/src/identity-checkout.js:105), [src/identity-checkout.js:108](C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/src/identity-checkout.js:108)

   A voucher signed by the previous on-chain signer fails current-signer recovery before reaching confirmed-expiry replacement. Even after it is provably expired and unused, `characterClaim` never invokes the safe replacement logic in `requestDynastyMint`.

   Correction: for signer mismatch, allow only the existing confirmed-expired-and-unused replacement path; continue rejecting live or uncertain vouchers.
