### H-01 — Production planner accepts unauthorized immutable market settings

[planner](/C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/tools/genesis-auction-deployment-plan.js:235) accepts any `poolFee` through `100000` and nonzero opening fees/caps. Preflight merely verifies those input-selected values.

### H-02 — Signer client does not bind governance policy to hook calldata

[client](/C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/public/genesis-deploy-client.js:53) checks `finalManifest.hookGovernance`, but [deployment validation](/C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/public/genesis-deploy-client.js:65) only requires nonempty hook calldata and packet-supplied runtime SHA.
