## Findings

### F-01 — Medium, release-blocking: supply-drift admission can be bypassed on-chain

The backend correctly rejects new bids when `OMR.totalSupply()` differs, while allowing recovery ([genesisauction.js](C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/src/genesisauction.js:161)). That restriction is not enforced by the auction:

1. OMR’s configured minter can change supply ([OMR.sol](C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/omerta-contracts/src/OMR.sol:125)).
2. The backend then blocks bid preparation.
3. An NFT holder can call `submitBid` directly.
4. The CCA invokes the validator, but the validator checks only chain, NFT code hash, recipient, and NFT balance ([GenesisCharacterEligibility.sol](C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/omerta-contracts/src/genesis-auction/GenesisCharacterEligibility.sol:23)).
5. The bid and ETH are accepted despite the required supply halt.

Impact: an unprivileged bidder can continue the sale after supply drift, invalidating the approved live-supply 40/20/40 allocation. The prerequisite is a privileged mint, but the direct bidder is the concrete unprivileged amplifier.

Fix: make the bid validator immutably bind OMR, its runtime hash, and the approved supply, and check `totalSupply()` only in `validate()`. Exit, refund, claim, and recovery paths do not invoke that hook and remain available.

### F-02 — Low, release-blocking for the zero-residual requirement: forced/PositionManager ETH can remain trapped

Migration correctly credits all accepted proceeds not spent on LP:

```text
residualCredit[Safe] += proceeds - spent
```

However, `_initializeLiquidity()` may also contain:

- ETH forced into the coordinator before migration; and
- preexisting PositionManager ETH swept to the coordinator.

The spend calculation deliberately excludes those amounts, but the credit at [OmertaAuctionCoordinatorV2.sol](C:/Users/Jorge/Documents/Omerta/output/contract-red-team-release/omerta-contracts/src/market-v2/OmertaAuctionCoordinatorV2.sol:206) includes only accepted proceeds. After the Safe withdraws its credit, extraneous ETH remains, with no native-dust recovery function.

Accepted auction proceeds are not lost; the defect concerns forced/periphery residue. Fix by tracking and sweeping surplus over outstanding Safe credit, including residue forced after migration.
