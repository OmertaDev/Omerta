# Economic pilot and expansion gate

The approved policy requires a genuine **90-day pilot**, followed by at least **90 further days of cash runway under stress**. A local test cannot compress that elapsed period or establish product health. `tools/tokenomics-pilot.js` produces an offline report for owner review; it cannot activate modules, spend funds, authorize expansion, or manufacture operational history.

Run `node tools/tokenomics-pilot.js PUBLIC_INPUT.json` to print a report. Add `--require-expansion` for exit 1 while any expansion gate is blocked; invalid input exits 2. The input schema is [tokenomics-pilot.schema.json](tokenomics-pilot.schema.json). Supply **public aggregate measurements only**, without credentials, personal identities, wallet keys, private database URLs, or player-level histories. The tool has no RPC client, database connection or signer. Evidence hashes identify externally retained, redacted measurement exports and exact read-only execution simulations; the tool does not authenticate their contents.

The policy freezes 50% of accepted Genesis ETH for initial LP, the 3.5% base trade fee split as 127/51/76/96 basis points, a separate mandatory 0.5% rewards charge on buys and sells, and the existing maximum 0.5% hook surcharge. The manager collects the base and rewards independently, preserving the original base allocation. Inventory bonds start dormant; any future Safe activation requires separately reviewed stress evidence, a discount no larger than 0.5%, and at least 30 days of linear vesting. This pilot enables no legacy city, Vig or pass emissions.

Stress assumes zero new players, zero reward top-ups, zero arbitrage income, volume **75% below plan**, **25% of claimed sale OMR offered in the first week**, and **all eligible bond OMR sold**. These are stress assumptions, not a prediction or a requirement to refuse actual new players. Do not count new-player income when evaluating the zero-growth case.

`plannedNativeFeeBaseWeiPerDay` means only the native-currency raw swap deltas that can actually produce ETH-denominated fees, not both directions' gross volume converted into ETH. The report shows a stressed operations-fee illustration but excludes all future fees from runway. Runway counts actual ETH outside the LP after every reserved native liability; LP principal, OMR inventory, unrealized token fees and expected future revenue cannot fund the calculation. Daily burn is the larger of the budget and the highest observed daily operating spend. The stress spends 90 days of that burn before checking the additional 90-day requirement. A zero or missing cost baseline cannot create infinite runway. Fiat reserves are conservatively excluded until an independently reviewed conversion and liability policy exists.

The exit model uses only the supplied **locked protocol LP range**, treats OMR as currency1, rounds output down and charges up, includes the LP input fee and the full 4.5% protocol ceiling, and stops at the range's upper price boundary. Base, mandatory rewards and maximum stability are separate charges, each rounded up conservatively. It ignores other LPs, replenishment, arbitrage, future buyers and favorable routing. The owner approved a maximum **40% all-in exit slippage (4000 basis points)**. Each report must retain its approval reference and may select a stricter limit; the schema rejects anything above 4000. A separately reviewed exact-input simulation for the complete stressed sale is mandatory. Its observedAt must be no older than one hour, never future-dated, and its block number/hash must equal the liquidity snapshot. Expiry is at most one hour after observation and must remain unexpired against both asOf and the actual CLI clock; the lesser of its minimum output and the modeled output is used. A model alone never clears the gate. Actual execution can differ through competing transactions, price movement, changing liquidity or external failures; this tool gives no execution or return guarantee.

Provide 90 consecutive UTC daily aggregate exports, each with its own retained evidence hash. Retention evidence records the original paying cohort, retained day-90 payers and repeat payers; the owner must separately approve minimum retention and repeat-payer criteria and reference that decision. Missing time, records, retention, report approval, claim backing, fresh cash/liquidity evidence, range capacity, simulation, slippage capacity or runway blocks expansion. The report itself may be at most one hour old against the actual CLI clock and may never be future-dated. Snapshot freshness is also bounded to one hour relative to the report's explicit `asOf`. Supplied dates and evidence remain assertions requiring independent verification before owner review.

Initial acceptance means **readiness to conduct the pilot**, not that it has succeeded. Before expanding, review paying-user retention without subsidy, repeat utility, payer concentration, bot reward share and actual costs alongside the report. Publish only finite funded campaigns, with no personal APY or guaranteed-profit claims. Keep bonds dormant and retain the current launch restrictions until their separate activation gates are satisfied.
