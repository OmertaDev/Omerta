# Restricted OMERTÀ market bootstrap

The mandatory 3.5% base trade charge and separate 0.5% WBTC rewards charge require a new `OmertaRestrictedOMRV2` token, `OmertaPoolManagerV2` settlement manager, and PositionManager paired to that manager. The manager caps positive hook surcharges at 0.5%, enforcing a maximum 4.5% protocol charge per swap across reviewed hooks; LP fees are additional. It cannot be installed by changing the hook or adding a PoolManager address to an existing ERC20 tax registry. This is a source-level deployment candidate; no production activation or migration has happened.

## Settlement and custody policy

The manager charges each ETH/OMR swap in both directions, including alternative unhooked pools. Exact-input swaps receive less output; exact-output swaps owe more input. It accounts actual deltas and partial fills, splitting the 350-bps base revenue 127/51/76/96 bps between founder/operations (`dev`), RWA, community and POL. The founder/operations share is 1.27%, with 0.51% for RWA, 0.76% for community and 0.96% for POL; base rounding residual belongs to POL. A separate `floor(abs(rawUnspecifiedDelta) * 50 / 10000)` accrues to the immutable WBTC rewards vault in manager bucket 4. The canonical hook adds only its separate opening or surge charge, bounded to 50 bps, and does not collect rewards twice. The manager independently rejects positive post-swap hook charges above that 50-bps bound, including charges from approved alternative hooks. Adding or withdrawing liquidity incurs neither mandatory trade charge.

The token's public policy ID is `keccak256("OMERTA_RESTRICTED_LP_REVENUE_V2_350_BASE_50_BTC_REWARDS_BOTH_DIRECTIONS")`. `bindSettlementManager(address,bytes32)` validates the manager's token, total 400-bps rate and policy ID. `setOperator(address,uint8,bytes32)` pins the actual deployed runtime. The enum is `None = 0`, `SettlementInput = 1`, `ProtocolCustody = 2`, `SettlementManager = 3`; role 3 is assigned only by manager binding. `setContractHolder(address,bytes32)` separately registers a runtime-pinned receiving-only contract. It grants no transfer or settlement authority. The genesis Safe is pinned through immutable `genesisTreasury`/`genesisTreasuryRuntimeHash` and is not an operator or an enumerated contract holder.

After `sealPolicy()`, existing operator roles and runtime pins, existing holder pins, the settlement manager and fee policy cannot be changed or revoked. New operator or receiving-only holder admissions require a Safe proposal, an immutable seven-day delay and revalidation of the deployed runtime at activation. The Safe can cancel a pending proposal; proposing does not grant transfer or receiving authority. A settlement input can transfer OMR only into the bound manager. A custody contract can receive into and distribute from its own address, and the manager can distribute only its own OMR; each recipient must also be allowed by the receiving policy. Standard wallet approvals work with `OmertaSwapRouterV2`; plain wallet transfers fail after sealing. Direct undelegated EOA traders are enrolled by the router. `registerWallet(address)` also lets an undelegated EOA register itself before or after sealing, with that same wallet as transaction origin. Smart wallets and contract treasuries require explicit receiving-only holder pins before receiving OMR, either in the initial registry or through the delayed admission process. These are substantial compatibility restrictions.

The Safe uses `proposeOperator(address,uint8,bytes32)` or `proposeContractHolder(address,bytes32)`. Anyone may execute the unchanged published proposal through `executeOperatorProposal(address)` or `executeContractHolderProposal(address)` once `POLICY_UPDATE_DELAY == 7 days` has elapsed; each execution rechecks the deployed code. `cancelOperatorProposal(address)` and `cancelContractHolderProposal(address)` are Safe-only. Replacing a pending proposal restarts the full delay. Existing receiving-only holders cannot be promoted to operators. Check the proposal's role, runtime hash and executable time independently; initial launch requires `pendingPolicyUpdates() == 0`.

Register the hook, stability controller, inventory bond and Turf registry as `ProtocolCustody`. Register the swap router, arbitrage contract, paired PositionManager and Permit2 as `SettlementInput`. Register POL funding, reserve funding, the Turf fee bridge and the WBTC rewards vault as mandatory receiving-only contract holders. They use reviewed typed approvals and do not need operator authority. The WBTC converter receives no OMR operator or holder permission. Other initial game custody contracts need their own explicit review and runtime pin before sealing. Later seasons and replacements require reviewed new controller/bridge contracts, completed delayed admissions, separate immutable budgets and explicit funding. Old controller limits never reset; settle its existing claims and principal obligations through its own reviewed paths. Arbitrary forwarding contracts, proxy implementations that can change underneath an operator pin, or general-purpose settlement operators undermine the restriction and must not enter the release policy.

PoolManager ERC6909 claims cannot be transferred or traded as substitute OMR. Unsupported deployed contracts and counterfactual contract addresses cannot directly receive native OMR through reviewed operators. The supported native LP guarantee excludes wrapped claims, custody IOUs, centralized books, internal ledgers and EIP-7702 delegation after a wallet has received tokens. The restricted manager admits only ETH/OMR pools and compatible registered hooks; operator or receiving-holder approval does not authorize independent managers.

## Staged deployment

1. Pin the reviewed source revision, compiler artifacts, chain snapshot, public roles and nonce owners. Before any deployment, verify the pinned eight-decimal WBTC and distinct external manager, the exact hookless native ETH/WBTC pool, and executable depth for the selected conversion budget. Initialization and nonzero active liquidity alone do not prove that depth. Predict the fourteen companion addresses using the explicit starting nonce: the rewards vault and converter precede the other twelve. Both use CREATE, allowing their future addresses and constructor-specific runtimes to be calculated without a CREATE2 hash cycle. `polFunding` and `btcRewards` are needed in the manager constructor. A separate prerequisite deployment account avoids consuming companion nonces; if an account is reused, account explicitly for every prerequisite deployment before selecting `startingNonce`.
2. Deploy `OmertaRestrictedOMRV2(treasurySafe)`. Its fixed 100,000,000 OMR supply is minted to the Safe and must remain there until sealing. Set `roles.capitalProvider` to that same Safe. No pool, independent wallet or protocol custody contract may be prefunded before the seal: pre-seal funding of a foreign manager could create internal claims that trade after sealing without a token transfer. `sealPolicy()` rejects any genesis inventory outside the owner.
3. Deploy `OmertaPoolManagerV2(omr,owner,recipients,rewardsRecipient)`, with the original recipient array in founder/operations (`dev`)/RWA/community/POL order, POL equal to the predicted `polFunding` address, and `rewardsRecipient` equal to `btcRewards`. Deploy the reviewed PositionManager against this manager and the selected Permit2. A PositionManager bound to a public Uniswap manager is incompatible.
4. Supply the replacement token, custom manager, paired PositionManager, Permit2, WBTC and external reward manager as `external.omr`, `external.poolManager`, `external.positionManager`, `external.permit2`, `external.wbtc` and `external.rewardManager`, each with reviewed actual runtime hashes. Supply the exact reward pool's fee and tick spacing under `policies.rewards`, and the constructor-specific converter runtime under `rewards.converterRuntimeHash`. The public Genesis wrapper derives that runtime field from `runtimeHashes.btcConverter` and emits nineteen total deployments. Generate the offline companion plan with schema 2. It emits unsigned inner Safe calls and direct public-caller transactions; it never contacts RPC, reads a signer, signs, simulates or broadcasts.
5. Deploy every companion and any additional reviewed custody/receiving contract. Complete its one-time game, season, bridge and controller bindings. Before manager binding, verify token ownership and policy ID, `operatorCount() == 0`, `contractHolderCount() == 0`, genesis Safe runtime identity, and that the Safe's balance equals the entire fixed supply with zero external inventory; manager OMR, total fee, original four recipients and rewards recipient; PositionManager manager/Permit2 pairing; every constructor binding and actual operator/holder runtime. The POL and rewards recipients must match the plan's deployed contracts. Verify the vault's Safe, fixed conversion dependencies and exact pools, and the converter's authorized vault and pinned runtime hashes.
6. Execute manager binding, operator registration and receiving-only holder registration as Safe calls. Fee and family treasury wallets that are neither genesis, operators nor approved holders self-register through direct unsigned EOA calls; verify live code is empty and the caller equals the transaction origin. Immediately before `sealPolicy()`, confirm the full supply still belongs to the Safe and compare both complete ordered registries. `operatorCount()` must equal nine plus additional operators; `operatorAt(index)`, `operatorRole` and `operatorRuntimeHash` must match manager first, eight required operators, then additions. `contractHolderCount()` must equal four plus additional holders; `contractHolderAt(index)` and `contractHolderRuntimeHash` must match POL funding, reserve funding, Turf bridge, rewards vault, then additions. Both enumerations include all ever-configured entries, including revoked ones; extra historical entries stop bootstrap. Verify every configured treasury is an allowed recipient. All fourteen companion deployments precede these calls, and sealing precedes pool initialization, bootstrap approvals, LP minting and controller/bond funding. A faulty sealed policy cannot be repaired by an owner update; stop if any binding, inventory balance, registry entry or runtime differs.
7. Initialize the canonical pool through its authorized initializer, seed the explicitly funded bootstrap PositionManager position, revoke temporary allowances and wait for a complete healthy observation epoch. Fund the declared controller tranches and bond inventory through their typed calls. Reproduce the whole sequence on the intended chain fork and retain resulting balances, deltas and runtime/configuration evidence before release authorization.

Configure upkeep revenue jobs as `manager_claim` with the manager artifact/runtime and buckets 0–4, for each required currency; bucket 4 delivers the rewards allocation to the fixed vault. Keep canonical `hook_claim` bucket 4 for opening/surge charges. POL delivery requires a manager bucket-3 sweep before the Core funding adapter's `funding_flush` can forward those assets. Conversion and epoch publication are separate Safe operations. The offline `tools/wbtc-reward-epoch.js` builder requires complete time-weighted wallet and backed-game observations and measured unreserved WBTC funding. Its `root`, `manifestHash` and `totalRewards` map to `publishEpoch`'s root, digest and budget. Published roots cannot be replaced, allocated rewards cannot be withdrawn, and claims do not expire. Merkle proofs establish inclusion, while the Safe remains responsible for the truth of the eligibility manifest.

For a later controller, the Safe calls the funding adapter's `proposeController(address,bytes32)` after reviewing its constructor and actual runtime. `CONTROLLER_UPDATE_DELAY` is seven days; anyone may execute the unchanged proposal through `executeControllerProposal()` afterward, and the Safe may cancel with `cancelControllerProposal()`. Replacing it restarts the delay. Execution independently requires the candidate's completed token `ProtocolCustody` admission and rechecks its Safe, token, manager, PoolKey, market-state source and compartment fee recipient against the existing controller. All assets already in the adapter flush to the old controller before the pointer changes; old principal, fee liabilities and spent limits stay with their contracts. Verify no pending funding-controller proposal at initial launch.

Every `readiness` step is a required live-verification condition, not an executed transaction or a verified assertion from the offline generator. Complete the independent chain reads and comparisons before the next transaction; preserve their evidence alongside the unsigned plan.

All LP and protocol inventory funding happens after sealing through the approved typed operators: the Safe approves Permit2/PositionManager for bootstrap and authorizes the controller and bond to pull their declared inventory. An independent capital provider would require a separate reviewed distribution path after sealing; this plan provides none and rejects that role assignment.

## Public manifest changes

The offline planner rejects schema 1 and any manifest without the explicit restricted policy. Add:

```json
{
  "schemaVersion": 2,
  "restrictedMarket": {
    "policyId": "<32-byte policy ID>",
    "operatorRuntimeHashes": {
      "hook": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "controller": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "bond": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "turf": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "arbitrage": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "router": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "positionManager": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "permit2": "<actual runtime hash>"
    },
    "additionalOperators": [],
    "holderRuntimeHashes": {
      "polFunding": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "reserveFunding": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "turfFeeBridge": "<actual runtime hash>",
      "btcRewards": "<actual runtime hash>"
    },
    "contractHolders": []
  }
}
```

All runtime inputs are public nonzero 32-byte hashes. PositionManager and Permit2 hashes must match their external pins. Additional operators require distinct public `address`, `role: 2`, `runtimeHash` and an explicit custody `purpose`. Additional receiving-only holders require distinct public `address`, `runtimeHash` and receiving `purpose`; they cannot duplicate an operator, mandatory holder or genesis Safe. A contract treasury for developer, RWA, community or family fees belongs in this holder list. The planner does not infer additional transfer permissions.

Artifact `runtimeTemplateKeccak256` values with unresolved immutable substitutions are not deployed runtime evidence. Constructor-specific runtimes must be reproduced and reviewed, then checked against the actual deployed code before registration and sealing. The local-chain example deliberately uses synthetic hashes and is unsuitable for a funded release. Set opening `buyBps` to at most 50: the manager's mandatory 350-bps base plus 50-bps rewards and the bounded canonical hook addition stay within the 450-bps ceiling, before LP fees and atomic-unit rounding. WBTC proxy runtime pins do not freeze bridge or issuer upgrade authority.

## Migration and release boundary

Existing OMR, LP positions, fee claims, bonds and funded game obligations remain on their existing contracts. Do not strand those claims by pointing existing clients at replacement contracts, asserting that old balances became restricted OMR, or withdrawing reserves before their obligations are discharged. A migration needs explicit allocation, custody, reserve and claim reconciliation; this bootstrap plan performs none of those actions.

Review the new token, custom manager, router, operator policy and their actual deployment configuration under the [agent-led security review policy](../../SECURITY-REVIEW-POLICY.md). Pin source revision, scope and release phase; retain targeted proofs, settlement tests, fuzzing/invariant evidence, findings and retests. An unsigned plan or passing source tests does not establish production activation or clear a funded release.
