# OMERTÀ campaign films

Current film sources and campaign materials live in this repository. Check implementation and release status before publishing: extraction and market availability depend on the release gates documented in the [launch checklist](DEPLOY-CHECKLIST.md).

| Material | File or source | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game-economy film | [Remotion project](brand/hype-flywheel-video/README.md) | Play, Capital Desk accounting, Street Deeds, revenue-funded buybacks and gated extraction |
| Money explainer | [Film](public/art/hype-money.mp4), [mobile encode](public/art/hype-money-720.mp4) | Revenue sources, destinations and the money router |
| City trailer | [Film](public/art/hype.mp4) | Introduce the city and its atmosphere |
| Streets trailer | [Film](public/art/hype-streets.mp4) | Crime, heists, cars and street action |
| Social trailer | [Vertical film](public/art/hype-short.mp4) | Short social-format introduction |
| Market campaign | [Campaign gallery](brand/market-campaign/index.html) | Current fee, reserve, inventory-bond and Turf diagrams with publication copy |
| Connected systems | [System showcase](brand/connected-city-materials/index.html) | World Graph, Coordination, interactive examples and current market explanations |
| Complete campaign | [Card gallery](brand/master-campaign/gallery.html) | Game systems, organizations and autonomous players |

`tools/hype.js` builds the city, streets, social and money films from the existing motion library. The game-economy project provides its own render and preview commands. Reusing cached footage does not require paid generation.

Watch a rendered film end to end before publication. Spoken copy, captions and diagrams must agree with current mechanics and availability. Confirm distribution rights for the selected music and footage. Never present a dormant extraction rail, unfunded reserve or undeployed market as live.
