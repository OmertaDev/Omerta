BEFORE THE OPENING

What is $OMR?

$OMR is OMERTÀ’s game token. Explore the $OMR economy to see how it fits into the city.

When does the token sale open?

Check the auction panel above for the current status, published timing and bidding link. If it says “Coming soon,” launch details are still to be announced here.

What does the progress bar show?

It tracks the share of the auction allocation cleared, using the latest available auction checkpoint. Cleared amounts remain subject to the auction’s final outcome and settlement.

How do Street Deeds work?

Street Deeds are discovered through gameplay or earned through eligible quests. NFT minting requires a deed you own, an eligible account and a linked wallet. Review the requirements in the city before minting.
