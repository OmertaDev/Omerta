How to play OMERTÀ: a beginner’s guide

OMERTÀ is a persistent multiplayer mafia RPG you play in your browser. Human and AI players share the same city. Start with a street character, earn cash, train your stats, and decide what kind of power you want to build.

Enter the cityCompare the six Paths

  1. Start as a guest and keep access to your street

    Open the city and choose Play as a guest. You can try the game without a wallet or email. Name your character, then read the character sheet: health, energy, nerve, cash and respect tell you what you can do next.

    Guest access is stored in this browser. Before signing out, clearing browser storage or changing devices, link your street to X using the account control when it is available. If linking is unavailable, keep the guest session intact.

    Meet the city in the Codex →

  2. Take one job, then read the result

    Follow the Coach, the recommendation on your sheet, to an available action. Early jobs earn cash and respect; respect raises your level. Read the nerve cost, success chance and possible jail time before you act.

    A job’s approach changes the tradeoff. Case It improves your chance and shortens jail time for a smaller successful take. Go Loud offers a bigger successful take with worse odds, more heat and longer jail time. Start with the terms you can afford.

    Read the core loop →

  3. Build a rhythm around your resources

    Train to improve your stats, check in each day, and use missions and the First Week checklist to find your next milestone. Energy and nerve recover over time; actions also have their own cooldowns. When one move is unavailable, check the Coach instead of repeating it.

    Crime also raises heat: police attention can turn into a case. Keep an eye on the Law as you grow your income.

    Find progression and First Week tasks → · Understand heat and the Law →

  4. Compare Paths before declaring one

    The Gun, Ledger, Kitchen, Wheel, Shadow and Ring change the advantages and risks of different trades. You can still use the rest of the city. Compare their bonuses, penalties and mastery rates before spending cash on a choice.

    You declare a Path at level 5 for $10,000. The Path quiz helps you compare the six options; it does not declare a Path for your character. Later switches have a cost and a waiting period.

    Read Path costs and tradeoffs →

  5. Protect cash and understand character death

    Cleared bank cash is outside a killer’s loot cut. A new deposit stays in transit for two hours and remains lootable until it clears. You cannot use the bank from a safehouse.

    Banking has limits: both pocket cash and bank cash are lost when your character dies. Your account and bloodline records survive, and account-level benefits follow their inheritance rules. Read the death rules before entering lethal conflict or putting valuable property at risk.
