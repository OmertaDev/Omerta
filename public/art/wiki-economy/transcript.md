# OMERTÀ economy explainer

Launch design; activation pending. Updated October 30, 2026.

## The city. The token. The stakes.

OMERTÀ connects a persistent crime game to an on-chain economy. Players build characters, earn cash, spend OMR, and compete over property. This film explains the current launch design and implementation. Production trading, staking rewards, NFT issuance, and stock delivery still require launch approval.

## Three currencies, different jobs.

Inside the city, cash pays for equipment, operations, and survival. OMR powers premium actions and commitments. Cash never buys OMR. ETH enters through supported market trades and paid services. Spending OMR recycles inventory; moving game balances on-chain requires verified backing.

## A fixed 100 million OMR.

OMR has a fixed supply of one hundred million tokens. Trading, gameplay accounting, and inventory programs move existing value. They do not create extra tokens. The launch plan auctions sixty million OMR. Total supply, circulating supply, and treasury inventory are different quantities.

## Where the 4% goes.

The base protocol fee is four percent. It splits across operations, the stock acquisition recipient, community, protocol liquidity, and ETH staking rewards, in the exact percentages shown here. Opening or sell surge can add half a percent. Liquidity provider fees are separate. Family turf can direct eligible future liquidity fees, never liquidity principal.

## Staking earns funded ETH.

On-chain OMR staking earns a share of ETH actually funded. Every deposit has its own thirty-day minimum withdrawal lock. Matured principal keeps earning until withdrawn. Earned claims and mature exits remain available during pauses. There is no promised fixed annual return.

## 2,500 permanent properties.

The city contains twenty-five hundred permanent Street Deeds: one thousand earned through gameplay, one thousand paid mints, and five hundred partnership properties. Earned discovery begins at level twenty-five. Both public routes create the same type of property, with the same operating rules and loss risks.

## The bloodline persists. The life takes risk.

Your soulbound identity NFT represents the bloodline. A separate escrow holds equipped Deeds for each mortal life. Equipped Deeds and their stock assets can pass to a player killer after recorded death and settlement. Safe release needs twenty-four hours' notice. Other deaths send inheritance to the exact heir after a separate twenty-four-hour estate delay.

## Stock rewards require real participation.

Stocks belong in the Deed's own token-bound account. New rewards require equipment on a living character, no pending exit, Broker activation, and qualifying play. Three activity tracks and a score of twenty-five determine eligibility for a proportional share of acquired stock. Purchases and deliveries require funded assets and enabled settlement.

## A game server and enforceable contracts.

The backend records authoritative gameplay; EVM contracts enforce on-chain custody and settlement. Node and Fastify serve the game, PostgreSQL stores its state, and viem connects wallets and contracts. Keepers connect confirmed game events with contracts. Dashboards must reflect the state actually observed.

## Built does not mean launched.

Implementation is different from activation. Current records show production database preparation and partnership reservations, but no contract deployment or minted partnership NFTs. Funding, verified configuration, indexing, and owner approval remain launch gates. Treat every reward flow as conditional until those gates open.
