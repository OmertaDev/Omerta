# WHAT EVEN IS OMERTÀ? — Four players, one problem

31.5 seconds · 1080×1920 · 30fps · Full-release launch promo

Read [fact matrix](00-FACT-MATRIX.md), [production bible](01-PRODUCTION-BIBLE.md) and [launch assumptions](02-LAUNCH-ASSUMPTIONS.md) with this package. Machine-readable timings and prompts are in [production-data.json](production-data.json).

## A. Concept

Four full-size criminals cannot get a gigantic polygon key around a concrete stairwell. The Rookie expected a quick mafia-game action. A stripped car becomes materials; earned knowledge makes a key craftable; four distinct people contribute; a human boss approves; the archive opens for the world. Smash-cut ELSEWHERE to a diverted shipment, a new market and an investigation. Phone fisheye, pavement inventory and industrial hardware make each new genre arrive as an interruption. The impossible prop is the joke; the requirements and consequences come from OMERTÀ.

**Factual boundary:** F01–F09, F12–F17, F20–F23, F26–F27. Four seats are from the Foundry pilot, not every mission. “Elsewhere” at 21.0 explicitly separates the campaign example from the archive. The shipment is diverted through an authorized operation; it is not a randomly disappearing commodity. Full-release master under L01 and L05; all pictured systems are available at publication.

## B. Viral thesis

- **recognition:** Group raids, inventory grids, mafia hierarchy, phone coordination.
- **novelty:** A gigantic key requires four full-size people and a real permission chain, then the city changes genre.
- **sensation:** Stairwell clang → pavement inventory → hydraulic press → four simultaneous calls → city shutter.
- **pattern interrupt:** Giant polygon key jammed at stairwell corner, FOUR adult carriers visible immediately.
- **information gap:** Why are other people and intel necessary for a door? The viewer learns the contributions before seeing the persistent payoff.
- **share trigger:** The friend who turns one quick quest into a raid group.
