Read [fact matrix](00-FACT-MATRIX.md), [production bible](01-PRODUCTION-BIBLE.md) and [launch assumptions](02-LAUNCH-ASSUMPTIONS.md) with this package. Machine-readable timings and prompts are in [production-data.json](production-data.json).

## A. Concept

A Rookie buys bulk cargo and ends up behind a tower of hard cases on a freight scale. Pickup becomes convoy, partial loss, a rival dock toll and Family cash. A full-size exchange pit interrupts: game cash is separate from OMR. Her canonical OMR sale brings in the Hook and POL/Core; a separate seasonal Turf lane becomes a rooftop territorial contest. She then gestures at an actual swimming pool while the Auditor explains that Family rights concern eligible funded fees and protocol principal stays protocol-owned. The exchange, rooftop and swimming pool are deliberately jarring editorial metaphors tied to precise game relationships.

**Factual boundary:** Full-release master under L01–L03 and L05. F10–F11, F24, F29, F32, F35–F38 and F42 apply. The Rookie owns the collected bulk cargo before dispatch; the staged ambush leaves a verified remainder. The destination is another Family’s territory, with the shipper present and funded for a positive toll. The canonical OMR sale is a separate human-confirmed action, not an automatic result of cargo trading. Hook POL funding goes to Core; designated seasonal Turf LP fees go through their own bridge. A siege settlement changes prospective rights, not historical credits or principal ownership. No tokenomics numbers, projected yield or invented receipt.

## B. Viral thesis

- **recognition:** Delivery disasters, guild turf, exchange terminals, crypto liquidity, literal pool joke.
- **novelty:** A cargo pickup becomes a separate OMR/Hook system and a rooftop fight over prospective fee rights.
- **sensation:** Freight beep → axle-cam theft → wire-fence payment → exchange pit → rooftop court → swimming pool.
- **pattern interrupt:** Freight flash: Rookie nearly buried behind hard cases on industrial scale.
- **information gap:** Why does one cargo purchase end with a Family fighting over a section of a market chart?
- **share trigger:** The friend who will argue over what holding a liquidity territory actually owns.
