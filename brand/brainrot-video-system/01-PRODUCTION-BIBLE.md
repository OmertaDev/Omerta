## Captions, safety zones and exports

- Master 1080×1920, 30fps. Film 1 = 945 frames; films 2/3 = 990 frames. Use a 48kHz audio timeline.
- Conservative cross-platform essential area: x=96–900, y=280–1480. No essential word under the right control rail, top chrome or bottom account/caption UI. Platform interfaces vary: preview the exact upload screen for all four destinations before export approval. This rectangle is a house layout, not a claim of permanent platform specifications.
- Speech captions start at y=1040; context occupies y=1340–1470; 76–92px semibold sans, 5–6px charcoal stroke, two lines maximum. Use a licensed font already available to production; verify the license before redistributing its file. Use brand display type only for the final wordmark.
- Split long lines into 2–5 word pages; all spoken words remain covered. The timed JSON provides line windows; align word-level pages to the recorded take. Preserve punctuation, contractions and speaker changes. Do not silently drop function words merely to shorten captions.
- Emphasize one word per beat: scale 100→118→100% over 6 frames, at most 12px displacement. Yellow for the key fact, red only for a accusation/status correction. No rapid full-screen flash or strobe.
- Extra joke labels are distinct from subtitles. Make factual labels such as HOOK FEE → POL/CORE, TURF LP FEES and PERMITTED CONTEXT mobile-readable. The films contain no development, inactive-market or prototype labels.
- Render H.264 MP4, progressive, Rec.709 SDR, high-quality 1080×1920 encode, AAC stereo 48kHz. Suggested starting bitrate 12–20Mb/s; inspect gradients and text after upload compression. Also retain a ProRes/mezzanine master, separate dialogue/music/SFX stems and clean picture.
- Deliver burned-caption masters, clean masters, caption JSON and SRT; no platform watermark. Vertical versions share the edit; adjust caption position if a platform preview occludes it.

## Audio before editing

Record scratch dialogue first at the exact beat windows. Characters speak to one another, not to a marketing audience. No narrator announcing “features.” Keep vocal takes dry and close; use natural breath and one restrained crack on the Rookie's exasperated line. For the human-form Consigliere call persona, use a dry telephone EQ and one restrained connection pip; keep words intelligible.

Commission or use commercially licensed original loops: film 1 scuffed 136 BPM breakbeat; film 2 broken 140 BPM electro plus abrupt pool room tone; film 3 sparse 104 BPM two-note suspense plus phone/copyshop artifacts. No recognizable melody, copyrighted film score, trending song or sampled game UI. Record/construct notification pips, paper tears, inventory ticks and mechanical impacts yourself or use cleared libraries. Sound-alike imitation of a named artist is not the brief.

Target roughly −14 LUFS integrated, ≤−1dBTP as a practical house mix, not a platform guarantee. Dialogue intelligibility wins. Duck music 4–7dB under lines; bass hits must retain an audible midrange click on phone speakers. Each timeline row names its sync event. Intentional silence removes music/SFX, while any scheduled dialogue remains intelligible. A final 3-frame room-tone crossfade connects tail to opening; do not cut off spoken consonants for the loop.

## Modular generation and opening budget

Six generated base plates per film, 5 seconds each = 90 generated seconds for the masters. The actual edits use shorter selections, frozen poses, editorial inserts and occasional reused plates. Source duration is not on-screen duration. Downloaded Seedance sources are measured with ffprobe; the first accepted source is 720×1280, 24fps, 5.041667s and is conformed into the 30fps edit. Never stretch a 30-second generated take into the full advertisement.

For each critical opening, generate **three candidates of the same chosen hook**: same approved frame/action, separate requests (or recorded R2V seeds 41001/41002/41003, plus 100 per film). Then generate the three alternate-hook plates only when running the opening test: 4 seconds each; swap the first 1.5 seconds and its voice/caption, keep the rest identical. Default opening allocation: 9 master-opening takes ×5s plus 9 variants ×4s = **81 seconds**, versus 75 seconds for all ordinary plates. Budget therefore favors the first 1.5 seconds. Start with the 9 master-opening takes and 15 ordinary plates; avoid producing all alternates before the first edit exists.

Dollar cost is deliberately not locked to a stale public estimate. At execution save the account's current quote for stills, clips, reference input and any audio/upscale work; set a per-batch cap from that quote. An invalid clip gets one diagnosed retry, not unlimited blind regeneration. Change one cause at a time: action, framing, identity reference or model.

Manifest fields: film, shot, variant, prompt text/hash, model ID, schema check date, input image hashes, duration/resolution/audio settings, input seed when supported, returned seed, request ID, quoted/actual cost, output path, rights/source record, selected/rejected and reason. ## Edit order and acceptance

1. Record dialogue and place every line at its timeline window. If a line won't fit intelligibly, tighten the performance or use the supplied shorter alternate, not a 1.5× voice speed hack.
2. Build a rough cut from reference stills and exact caption JSON. This is an animatic, clearly labeled; no model spend required to test the joke.
3. Generate/select opening candidates, then ordinary plates. Retain candid micro-errors that don't confuse the action; reject identity drift, extra limbs and illegible pseudo-UI.
4. Capture required game panels from staged eligible accounts on the advertised release. Record the release build, prerequisites and before/after state. These films show group play and market mechanics, not a claim that a new solo account can bypass Family authority.
5. Composite real release UI, captions, recurring jokes and the OMERTÀ wordmark. The new polygonal human-form call persona is the Consigliere character; actual adviser captures use the same released world/Knowledge/command integrations. Human confirmation and scopes remain visible when they matter.
6. Cut/mix to the timecoded event map. Render muted and phone-speaker reviews: muted must still communicate the cause/effect; phone sound must carry the joke.
7. Verify no visual hold exceeds 2 seconds without an information change; contextual game states remain legible; no text under upload chrome; all displayed financial receipts are genuine release captures; no invented token values or definite betrayal accusation.
8. Export each master and three opening-only variants. Compare 1.5s/3s hold, completion, replays and shares per view at comparable distribution; do not promise virality or call a handful of views statistically decisive. Record confusion quality: “how does that work?” is useful; “what do Families actually control?” calls for checking the fee-rights/principal distinction.
