# Original animation art — prompt set

Built-in imagegen was used, including its true-alpha output for separate character and prop layers. The following is the normalized production prompt set; the project manifest records each returned original file.

Shared direction: sophisticated hand-painted adult noir animation, strong ink contours, textured materials, amber practical light and cool teal shadows. A rainy working harbor city. Original characters; no recognizable copyrighted characters. No interface, screenshots, generated text, logos, or watermarks.

- **Opening reference:** wide seated-eye view of Nico at a scarred wooden table, one sealed cream envelope, an empty chair opposite, amber desk lamp left and rainy harbor window right. Nico is early30s, olive complexion, short wavy dark hair, clean-shaven, charcoal wool jacket and cream open-collar shirt, no hat.
- **Opening background:** remove Nico, his hands and envelope; preserve table, chair, light and window.
- **Nico seated:** isolate the seated man, preserving identity, clothing, pose, scale and lighting; transparent environment.
- **Nico body:** remove the foreground bent sleeve and hand from the seated cutout, repaint only the revealed torso, preserve everything else.
- **Nico forearm:** isolate connected charcoal sleeve, cream cuff and relaxed hand, horizontally oriented from left elbow to right hand, suitable for an envelope push.
- **Envelope:** isolate cream paper envelope and burgundy wax seal at shallow tabletop perspective, no hands or table.
- **Nico standing:** same identity, full body, three-quarter side view facing right, charcoal jacket, cream shirt, dark trousers, worn brown shoes, left hand in pocket.
- **Mara standing:** athletic woman early30s, light brown complexion, auburn bob, wary expression, burgundy leather jacket, black high-neck shirt, charcoal trousers and practical boots. Full body facing left, folded arms.
- **Street:** empty damp road with bank facade, boxing gym doorway, café, repair garage and distant cranes. Clear space for actors and vehicle.
- **Car:** battered dark olive mid-century four-door sedan, side view facing right, both wheels visible, no people or branding, transparent background.
- **Dock:** warehouses, coffee crates, wet quay road, moored cargo ship, layered cranes. Empty foreground for a car and cargo.
- **Payroll Office:** stone industrial office with heavy arched door, amber entrance lamp, empty wet street, blind alley and cool moonlight. No actors, vehicles or police.
- **Prison:** bare holding-cell wall, cot, timber bench, high barred window casting moonlight; no foreground bars, which close in animation.
- **Evidence desk:** overhead worn mahogany table, lamp, analog camera, magnifying glass and pencil at edges; large empty center for animated papers.
- **Family room:** long walnut council table, empty leather chairs, rainy harbor windows and large generic city map in foreground; no labels or tokens.
- **Finance desk:** overhead counting-house desk, three separated compartments: empty wooden cash drawer, empty brass dish and closed dark steel safe box. No money or words.
- **Coffee sack:** one tied bulging burlap sack with a few coffee beans, worn seam, amber light, no writing; transparent background.
- **Evidence file:** worn buff folder seen from above, blank slips and one black paperclip; transparent background.
- **Coin:** one worn antique brass coin seen nearly top-down with blank center and milled edge, no symbols; transparent background.
- **Paths table:** six physical tools in two rows: revolver, ledger, pot; steering wheel, magnifying glass, boxing gloves. Dark wood, broad gaps, no text or UI cards.
- **Boat:** small working cargo motorboat side view facing right, blue hull, cream cabin, crates aft and tire fenders; no water or background.
- **Equipment:** open leather satchel with protective vest, binoculars and rope, three-quarter top view, no cash or firearms; transparent background.

All selected project assets are saved under `public/art/flagship-animation/`. Character layers were registered in the compositor after generation; original alpha channels were preserved. Diagnostic contact sheets are review aids, not film artwork.
