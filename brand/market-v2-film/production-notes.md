# OMERTÀ Market V2 — The City's Reserves

**New dedicated film: 2:53, vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.** This expands the economic overview in the previous three films. Its central story follows a market move through active inventory ranges, reversible conversion, actual withdrawal, consumed capacity, qualified observations and funded recovery. It then connects the funding sources, participants and Family entitlements without blending their custody.

## What this adds

- The roles of Core, Lower Cushion, Garrison, Upper Cushion, Desk and idle War Chest funding.
- Conversion while crossing active ranges; price reversal before withdrawal; eligible finalization; principal versus earned fees.
- Per-action, observation, episode and lifetime limits; completed market observations; stress thresholds, cooldowns and current-condition checks.
- A complete, spaced recovery window; actual idle assets; bounded partial refill; lifetime limits that remain consumed.
- Separate canonical fee buckets, additional sell pressure, opening-buy policy, actual fee currencies, partial fills and independent trading venues.
- Prefunded inventory bonds and independent vested claims; solver-funded commit/reveal arbitrage and actual-profit checks.
- Custodied LP position NFTs, sampled useful depth, funded reward campaigns and independent maturity exit.
- Seasonal Turf slabs, syndicates, territorial status, atomic fee checkpoints, frozen siege wallets, expiry settlement, season exit and protocol principal custody.
- Safe binding/funding, typed game outcomes, constrained keepers, confirmation/reorg handling and separate ledgers.

The [fact matrix](fact-matrix.md) compares the previous film with **19 implemented capability groups and 36 detailed mechanism/condition rows**. It supplies precise source references and further implementation limits; the film is a clear mechanism explanation, not a contract walkthrough.

## Creative and audio

Recognition comes from a city reserve system, market ranges, a war chest and Family territory. The reveal is that every response consumes assets and permissioned capacity. The visuals make changing inventory legible before showing the conditions for another deployment.

The film uses cinematic black and ivory, copper and blue accents, original scope/360 transitions, measured phrase captions and an original mechanical-noir score. Intentional instrumental silence marks depleted capacity and conditional recovery. Original generated narration uses the campaign's Auditor voice.

At execution, the authenticated fal catalog showed MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD active. A pilot confirmed the account's original voice worked with it. Its [official input schema](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/minimax/speech-2.8-hd/api) supports the prompt and voice settings used. The queried price was $0.10 per 1,000 characters; this is the model's listed rate, not an account billing total. Narration inputs and result hashes are in the generation ledger. No API credential is stored in the source package.
