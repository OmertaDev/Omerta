## Authority and exits

The Safe controls the bounded governance actions; the game adjudicator settles typed outcomes, not arbitrary capital calls. Placement is permissionless but deterministic: callers do not choose price or amounts. Fee collection, owner exits, matured commitment withdrawals, vested bond claims and Turf expiry intentionally have different dependencies from new strategy/purchase actions. Do not compress all of these into a universal “pause freezes everything” switch.
