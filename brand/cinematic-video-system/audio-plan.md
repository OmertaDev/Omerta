# OMERTÀ cinematic campaign — audio plan

All three scores and effects are newly synthesized for this campaign. No third-party samples or previous-campaign audio are reused. The narration uses original account-designed voices with newly generated words. This plan records the actual local mix, not a proposed soundtrack.

Technical measurements do not replace listening review for pronunciation, delivery, music timbre or phone-speaker balance.
