The Hook / Market

A canonical ETH / OMR market with rules for fees, reserves and family interests.

2%Developer1.6%RWA recipient2.4%Community3%Protocol liquidity

Bounded surge and opening protections live in the Hook. Companion contracts manage finite reserves, vesting inventory bonds, solver-funded arbitrage, seasonal Turf fee rights and real liquidity commitments.

Base sell fee: 9%. Extra surge: 0–1%. LP fees are additional. Hook taxes apply to the canonical pool; independent pools have their own policies.

Implementation candidate · The market is not deployed or funded

Independent sources → Shared conclusion

Private investigations, immutable discoveries and deliberate sharing with an account, Crew or Family. Permissions are checked when evidence is read or used. A copy cannot become another independent source.
