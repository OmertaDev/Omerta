Fresh funding and position removal do not themselves restore episode capacity. Eligible recovery can replenish a bounded episode amount from actual idle War Chest assets. Lifetime deployment remains consumed.

A price rebound does not create ETH. Finite ranges can reverse before removal; a chart crossing a wall is not a finalized inventory event.

Some exits remain independent.

The runbook separates new controller deployment from collection, claims and exits. Mature commitment withdrawals, already vested bond claims and funded Family claims do not become new deployment authority. Read each contract’s path rather than assuming a pause freezes everything.
