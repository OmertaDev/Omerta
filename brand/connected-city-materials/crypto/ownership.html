Who controls what?

An object, an account, a fee right and a governance permission are different things. Follow the authority attached to each.

IMPLEMENTED PHASE 1

World Graph inventory

Authority or custody
Recorded character/domain ledger owner
What that enables
The game’s item and escrow ledgers record custody and exact historical depositors.
Boundary
Phase 1 inventory is not an exported NFT. Death and replacement do not automatically rewrite ownership or transfer old instance authority.
DEFAULT-OFF API PILOTS

Original discoveries

Authority or custody
Discovering account; deliberate sharing grants
What that enables
Owners share knowledge with an account, Crew or Family; current permissions govern future reads and actions.
Boundary
Sharing, copying and archiving do not create another original source. A shared reader is not a new original discoverer.
MARKET CANDIDATE

Family Turf fees

Authority or custody
Authorized treasury / funded credits
What that enables
A Family’s lane ownership determines prospective fee allocations. Already credited balances remain with their resolved recipients.
Boundary
Turf ownership gives no right to remove LP principal. A Family member is not automatically the credited treasury.
MARKET CANDIDATE

Protocol liquidity principal

Authority or custody
Controller custody; constrained Safe authority
What that enables
The controller records principal separately from earned fees. The Safe has pause, exit and retirement operations governed by the contracts.
Boundary
The game adjudicator cannot withdraw controller principal, invent fees or submit arbitrary calls through the settlement adapter.
MARKET CANDIDATE

Committed liquidity NFT

Authority or custody
Vault custody during the agreed term
What that enables
An eligible actual canonical PositionManager NFT is locked. The depositor has a maturity exit independent of reward availability.
Boundary
Ownership outside the vault cannot bypass the lock. Rewards are prefunded, bounded and subject to qualifying observations.
MARKET CANDIDATE

Inventory-bond entitlement

Authority or custody
Buyer’s nontransferable vesting note
What that enables
A funded purchase reserves its complete OMR entitlement; vested claims have an independent claim path.
Boundary
This is an inventory sale, not Family debt, a transferable bond market, or a promise of returns.

The powers behind the map

Fixed in the Hook

Recipient addresses, base rates, maximum surge and opening policy are immutable. The opening window cannot be extended by an administrator. The canonical Hook has no discretionary trading pause.

Operational authority remains

The Safe configures seasons and ranges before they start, and controls specific controller operations. The adjudicator submits bounded, typed game outcomes. These are meaningful trust boundaries, not a claim that the whole system is administrator-free.

A fee allocation to an RWA recipient proves a routing destination. It does not, by itself, establish legal ownership of an underlying asset or a personal redemption right.
