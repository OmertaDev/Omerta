What a live receipt must contain

Chain ID, canonical PoolKey, verified Hook address, transaction hash, block and confirmation state; the actual fee currency and FeesAccrued / FeesPaid logs; recipient addresses and reserve funding evidence. Accrual, payment and adapter flush can be separate transactions.

Those fields are deliberately absent here. The candidate is not presented as deployed or funded. When a deployment is verified, contract and explorer links belong beside the matching evidence—not beside invented addresses.
